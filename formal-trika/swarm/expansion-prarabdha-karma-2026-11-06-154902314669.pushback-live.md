CLM-001 through CLM-008 are all R-tier reconstructions, and their own locators—`tsa-a08-v20`, `tsa-a08-v21`, `tsa-a08-v23`—have “no formal entry” in the banked evidence. CLM-005 (“Action mere; passion mind-nature; distinction deferred”) and CLM-008 (“Knowledge-kevalin starves action; dissolution-kevalin fuels it”) are especially editorial: they convert technical distinctions into agentive slogans.

The strongest counter-reading is that the passage is not a standalone definition “passion = bounded longing.” It is a technical discrimination within a mala/karma cosmology: `buddhidharmas tu rāgaḥ` more naturally means “rāga is a dharma/property of buddhi,” not “mind-nature is passion”; `karmāvacchinna` means “delimited/conditioned by karman,” not necessarily “action-bounded” in the definitional sense claimed; `jṛmbhamāṇa` may be “expanding/arising,” not “yawning”; and `āpyāyati` means “nourishes/increases,” not “fattens” or “starves” as a dramatic metaphor. The verses may distinguish `karman` as `karmamātra` from `rāga` as a `buddhi`-property, while deferring a fuller `karmabheda` account. That is not the same as CLM-001/002’s “passion-principle is action-bounded longing” as a doctrinal definition.
