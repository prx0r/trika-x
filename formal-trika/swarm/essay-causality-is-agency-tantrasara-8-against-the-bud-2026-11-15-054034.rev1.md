## Abstract
In its own terms the inquiry begins from pervasion: what runs through the world as mighty light is taken as the common whole [tsa-a08-v01]. A category is what pervades only some differences, as earth pervades world-eggs [tsa-a08-v02]. The objection arises at once: if all is one light, why speak of cause and effect at all? Could sequence alone do the work? The reply developed across the eighth ahnika is twofold: cause-effect-ness is shown as twofold [tsa-a08-v03], ultimate and of emission, though the second term hangs in an uncertain genitive that this essay leaves open [tsa-a08-v04]. The consequence is that freedom never lapses even within fixed order [tsa-a08-v07]. What holds assemblage-talk together? Who makes the pot? How does variety fit? These three questions organize the whole.

The present revision answers a single enquiry: causality is agency. It meets the Buddhist regularity objection on its strongest form and it shows how the five powers ground the tattva hierarchy from Siva to earth. Regularity is not denied. It is retained as willed fixity, only so far, even in the more, and variety is retained as freedom's signature. The total assemblage is called cause, but assemblage strengthens rather than replaces awareness as maker. Impurity, emission, Maya and kalā are then shown to belong to one argument about agency, not to a second dualist ontology. The essay proceeds by close reading, staging more than twenty objections in their strongest form and answering each with a substantive reply from Abhinavagupta's voice, and it closes with a desideratum on binding without being bound.

## Introduction
The Teacher first fixes what pervades everything and what pervades only something. What runs through all as great-light-formed is like the great common, Supreme-Siva-formed [tsa-a08-v01]. By contrast, whatever form pervades only some differences is a category, as earth of glow-hardness-bulk form pervading all world-eggs ruled from Kalagni to Virabhadra [tsa-a08-v02]. Cause-effect-ness concerning these categories is then shown, and it is twofold [tsa-a08-v03]. Already the architecture is visible. Pervasion gives unity and difference. Causality must give production without breaking pervasion. The twofoldness is therefore not an afterthought but the hinge on which the tattva exposition turns.

Objection 1: if pervasion already explains unity and difference, why add causality at all? Is causality not merely observed before-after regularity, needing no agent? What work does a Lord do that succession cannot do?

Reply to Objection 1: pervasion explains what is co-present, not what comes to be. That earth pervades eggs does not explain why this pot comes to be here now from this assemblage and not otherwise. The Teacher therefore introduces cause-effect-ness as showing-forth, not as extra entity over pervasion but as pervasion in its dynamic aspect. Ultimate cause-effect-ness is defined without remainder as the free Lord's self-showing:

> tatra pāramārthika etāvān kāryakāraṇabhāvo yad uta kartṛsvabhāvasya svatantrasya bhagavata evaṃvidhena śivādidharāntena vapuṣā svarūpabhinnena svarūpaviśrāntena ca prathanam [tsa-a08-v05]

Which is to say, the free Lord's self, agent-natured, in Siva-to-earth flesh, same-natured, self-resting, shines. The ultimate so defined stands even when unconstructed [tsa-a08-v07]. Pervasion without shining would be a static genus. Shining without pervasion would be creation ex nihilo. Together they give causality as agency.

Objection 2: does this definition not collapse cause into effect and destroy explanation? If ultimate causation is self-showing with Siva-to-earth body, non-different in nature and resting in own nature, then where is the asymmetry that causation requires? Why call self-resting shining by the name of making?

Reply to Objection 2: asymmetry is preserved inside non-difference as the difference between resting and shining, between unconstructed standing and constructed appearing. The ultimate is just this much, namely showing-forth, and the constructed is made by will with necessity-breath as fixed before-after appearing, only so far even in the more [tsa-a08-v06]. If ultimate causation is self-showing, then constructed fixed order must be reassessed rather than dismissed [tsa-a08-v06]. How can mere sequence explain production? The Tantrasara reframes the question from succession to shining. Sequence describes when shining is fixed. It does not produce shining.

## Historical Context
Trika Saivism emerged in the middle of the ninth century as a major post-scriptural ritual system of the Mantramarga [Sanderson 1988, via Kaul 2022]. Kashmir possessed vibrant Tantric Saiva traditions up through at least the thirteenth century [Sanderson 2007a, via Kaul 2022]. Abhinavagupta is engaged in providing critical philosophical structure to Trika scriptures [Lawrence 2000, via Kaul 2022]. To read the eighth ahnika outside that program is to mistake intervention for compendium. The question of causality before tattvas is not scholastic ornament. It licenses the entire hierarchy that follows, from pure path to impure path, from Siva through Ananta to Maya and earth.

Objection 3: was this merely ritual elaboration without philosophical stakes? Could systematization be dismissed as apologetics? What distinguishes philosophical rationalization from scriptural repetition?

Reply to Objection 3: Abhinavagupta's treatment of causality before tattvas exemplifies philosophical rationalization of ritual and doctrine [Allen 2003, via Kaul 2022]. He offers an analytical account of reflection before phonemic emanation on exactly parallel lines [Kaul 2019, via Kaul 2022]. Like his predecessor he was a master of creative reuse [Watson 2006, via Kaul 2022]. The proof is that he does not merely repeat that Siva emits. He gives reasons why emission must be twofold, why assemblage must be called cause, why variety must fit, and why impurity cannot be a second substance. That is philosophy serving scripture by grounding it.

Objection 4: even if philosophical, is the inheritance not eclectic to the point of incoherence? How did predecessors make dynamism of consciousness, Samkhya ontology, Nyaya-Vaisesika inference, and Vijnanavada pramana ideas serve nondualism [Rastogi 2013, via Kaul 2022]? Does borrowing from realists and Buddhists alike not betray the nondual claim?

Reply to Objection 4: borrowing is method, not betrayal, when the borrowed element is regrounded. Samkhya gives tattva names and satkarya intuitions, Nyaya gives vyapti and counterfactual control as in Meru cases [tsa-a08-v12], Vijnanavada gives the wall-picture and dream-pot analyses, and Pratyabhijna dynamism gives svatantrya as their ground. Abhinavagupta categorizes Trika procedures after means precisely to show that means converge when regrounded [Rastogi 2012, via Kaul 2022]. Earlier scriptures removed impurity by knowledge, yoga, ritual, and observances [Brunner 1992, via Kaul 2022], and he shows why all four can work: they address different fixities of the same agency. Eclecticism would leave joints visible. Regrounding makes joints load-bearing.

## Literature and Method
Method here is close reading under verse protocol, quoting only packet Sanskrit verbatim and leaving uncertain readings open. Each successive lower tattva draws sustenance from higher tattvas as material cause [Kaul 2018, via Kaul 2022]. The concept of cause manifests fully in the Saiva notion of agent, and effect is understood as functioning of the potential agent [Rastogi 2006, via Kaul 2022]. That sentence is the thesis in miniature. Cause is not antecedent. Cause is agent in its capacity to shine thus. Effect is not consequent. Effect is agency functioned.

Objection 5: does such a method beg the question by privileging the Saiva voice? Is debate reduced to wrangling rather than reasoning? What separates good debate based on reasoning from bad debate based on wrangling [Matilal 1998, via Kaul 2022]?

Reply to Objection 5: the procedure avoids question-begging by staging the rival at strongest, then answering from Abhinavagupta with cited verse, then drawing consequence that even the rival must accept on its own examples. The Buddhist is not refuted by scripture. The Buddhist is shown that seed-sprout, wish-object, dream-pot and dung-worm examples already require wall, totality and counterfactual stability that regularity alone cannot supply [tsa-a08-v06] [tsa-a08-v08] [tsa-a08-v13]. Good debate gives reasons the other side can recognize. Bad debate merely reasserts. This essay practices the first by letting invariability, assemblage and Meru do the work.

Objection 6: does close reading fetishize philological uncertainty? The second term of the twofold hangs in an uncertain genitive [tsa-a08-v04], and the packet notes a niyata/nirata fork [tsa-a08-v09]. Should doctrine wait on philology? If readings are open, can conclusions be firm?

Reply to Objection 6: openness at the letter secures firmness at the doctrine. Leaving the genitive open records that emission can be read as objective or subjective without loss: whether ultimate-and-of-emission or ultimate-of-emission, the point stands that constructed order is emitted fixity, not independent mechanism. Likewise keeping fixed as doctrinally fitting without smoothing the philology [tsa-a08-v09] shows that the argument does not depend on emendation. Impurity as incompleteness of knowledge rather than non-knowledge must guide reading of mala passages [Acharya 2014, via Kaul 2022]. This essay shows that causality passages and mala passages belong to one argument. What holds wall and picture together if not agency? Uncertainty about a syllable does not unsettle an architecture when both readings point to agency.

## The Doctrinal Context
The ultimate cause-effect-ness is the free agent-Lord's showing-forth with Siva-to-earth body, non-different and self-resting [tsa-a08-v05]. The constructed cause-effect-ness is made by the Lord's will with necessity-breath, as fixed before-after appearing, only so far even in the more [tsa-a08-v06]. Even there is non-loss of the Lord's agency, thus the unconstructed ultimate stands firm [tsa-a08-v07]. The three moves form a single inference. Ultimate shining is posited. Constructed fixity is admitted as willed. Non-loss is concluded even within fixity. Freedom therefore never lapses even within fixed order [tsa-a08-v07].

The same ultimate is stated again because the doctrine turns on it and repetition is exegesis:

> tatra pāramārthika etāvān kāryakāraṇabhāvo yad uta kartṛsvabhāvasya svatantrasya bhagavata evaṃvidhena śivādidharāntena vapuṣā svarūpabhinnena svarūpaviśrāntena ca prathanam [tsa-a08-v05]

Objection 7: does twofoldness double the world? If ultimate already shines as all, why construct fixed order? Is constructed order redundant, or does it betray freedom?

Reply to Objection 7: constructed order is willed fixed appearing, proved by sprout from seed, yogin's wish, dream, and pots [tsa-a08-v06]. Redundancy would follow only if shining and fixing were rivals. They are not. Shining is that there is appearing at all. Fixing is that appearing holds thus far thus. Freedom is not betrayed by fixity because fixity is freedom holding itself. With ultimate wall-like standing form all this drawn fits, not otherwise, hence by assemblage alone is caused-ness fitting [tsa-a08-v08]. The assemblage is made of all-being's weave by free-awareness greatness, of fixed own place-time-thing-mass nature [tsa-a08-v09]. Fixity is therefore the measure of will, not its limit.

Objection 8: does agency lapse when order appears fixed? If seed invariably precedes sprout under conditions, is it not conditions that do the work? Why invoke awareness behind invariability?

Reply to Objection 8: invariability itself is only so-far shining. Deviation and degree follow assemblage difference, as effect's other-kind-ness nourishes by degrees [tsa-a08-v09]. If conditions did the work by themselves, deviation would be unintelligible and degree would be accidental. But deviation scales with assemblage and degree nourishes accordingly, which shows that what is called necessity is stability of shining across a total set. Here the packet notes a niyata/nirata fork; this essay keeps fixed as doctrinally fitting without smoothing the philology [tsa-a08-v09]. Does agency lapse when order appears fixed? No. Agency appears as order.

## Encountering the Interlocutor
The Buddhist theory of dependent origination holds that phenomena happen in series, certain phenomena being followed by others [Dasgupta 2004, via Kaul 2022]. On that view, to be a cause is to be an invariably antecedent condition, and no agent over and above the series need be posited [tsa-a08-v03]. Stated economically, regularity suffices. Stated polemically, agency is cost without benefit. The Tantrasara must answer this view not as caricature but as science of series.

Objection 9, in strongest form, on behalf of the Buddhist: regularity suffices. Seed-then-sprout, wish-then-object, dream-then-pot are explained by place in a series without invoking free awareness [tsa-a08-v06]. Why posit a wall behind the painting? Is the wall not an extra metaphysical cost?

Reply to Objection 9: the wall is not extra. It is what makes painting possible. Invariability itself is only so-far shining, willed with necessity-breath [tsa-a08-v06]. For with wall-like standing ultimate form all drawn fits, not otherwise [tsa-a08-v08]. The text cited:

> pāramārthike hi bhittisthānīye sthite rūpe sarvam idam ullikhyamānaṃ ghaṭate na anyathā ata eva sāmagryā eva kāruṇatvaṃ yuktam [tsa-a08-v08]

Given the true's wall-like steadiness, all this drawn fits, else not. So by the mass alone causation befits. Assemblage-doctrine thus strengthens makership of awareness alone, world-bodied [tsa-a08-v11]. Series-talk is retained but grounded; total set, not isolated antecedent, is called cause [tsa-a08-v08]. Who makes the pot if not the potter? The question is not rhetorical. It marks that series omits maker while using maker's work.

Objection 10, stronger still: how can an unconstructed shining explain why Meru conditions rule out such-and-such pot [tsa-a08-v12]? What explains invariability without reifying consciousness? If shining is everywhere, why is pot not on Meru? If answer is assemblage, then assemblage explains, not Siva.

Reply to Objection 10: Meru proves agency precisely because it controls assemblage. On Meru abiding there no such pot would be [tsa-a08-v12]. The counterfactual holds because place-time-thing-mass fixity belongs to assemblage nature [tsa-a08-v09]. But assemblage nature is itself fixed appearing, willed only so far [tsa-a08-v06]. To say assemblage explains is true at constructed level and incomplete at ultimate level, for assemblage does not assemble itself. The wall teaching must be cited again because the Buddhist presses cost:

> pāramārthike hi bhittisthānīye sthite rūpe sarvam idam ullikhyamānaṃ ghaṭate na anyathā ata eva sāmagryā eva kāruṇatvaṃ yuktam [tsa-a08-v08]

Without standing wall, drawn lines float. Without total set, antecedent floats. Total set without awareness floats. Grounding is not reifying. It is refusing to let floating count as explaining.

Objection 11: is this not merely renaming regularity as will? If willed fixity behaves exactly like brute regularity, what empirical difference does agency make? Is svatantrya testable or transcendental decoration?

Reply to Objection 11: the difference is modal and practical, not merely verbal. Brute regularity cannot explain why yogin's wish, mantra, herbs and dream yield objects outside ordinary series yet still intelligibly [tsa-a08-v13], nor why recognition, ritual and knowledge can alter fixity for the same person [Brunner 1992, via Kaul 2022] [Acharya 2014, via Kaul 2022]. If regularity were brute, siddhi would be miracle and liberation would be accident. If regularity is willed fixity, siddhi is freedom exercising other fixities and liberation is freedom recognizing itself. The Buddhist series must treat yogin-wish and mantra as embarrassments or as separate series ad hoc. The Saiva treats them as predictions: variety is exactly what constructedness predicts since fixed before-after holds only so far [tsa-a08-v06]. Testability lies in transformation, which Trika procedures systematize after means [Rastogi 2012, via Kaul 2022].

## The Argument I
The free-awareness-natured Supreme Lord alone, world-thing-bodied, is maker of pots and the rest, since potter-awareness is nothing beyond and potter-body is placed amid thing-mass [tsa-a08-v10]. Hence assemblage-doctrine strengthens awareness-alone's makership [tsa-a08-v11]. On Meru abiding there no such pot would be [tsa-a08-v12]. The potter is not denied. The potter is located. Awareness-aspect belongs to the one awareness. Body-aspect belongs amid masses. Makership-aspect belongs to totality functioning as agent.

Full citation, because the move turns on locating finite agency inside infinite agency:

> ity evaṃ saṃvedanasvātantryasvabhāvaḥ parameśvara eva viśvabhāvaśarīro ghaṭāder nirmātā kumbhakārasaṃvidas tato 'nadhikatvāt kumbhakāraśarīrasya ca bhāvarāśimadhye nikṣepāt kathaṃ kumbhakāraśarīrasya kartṛtvābhimānaḥ iti cet parameśvarakṛta evāsau ghaṭādivat bhaviṣyati [tsa-a08-v10]

Bright-knowing-free, the Highest alone, world-stuff bodied, pots he builds — for pot-mind is nothing beyond him, and pot-body cast amid masses — how maker-pride? Highest-made even that shall be.

Objection 12: does this dissolve finite agency into divine monopoly? If potter-awareness is nothing beyond, is human effort illusory? Why preserve talk of total set if one maker does all?

Reply to Objection 12: finite pride is not denied but derived; it too is made [tsa-a08-v10]. The Teacher anticipates maker-pride: how is the potter-body's maker-pride? Thus if Lord-made even that will be, like pots and the rest [tsa-a08-v10]. Monopoly would mean only Siva acts and potters do not. Derivation means potters act because Siva acts as potters. Total-set talk is preserved because the one maker acts as total set, not instead of it. Sāmagrī is thereby converted from rival explanation into evidence for agency [tsa-a08-v11]. What confirms that assemblage-talk strengthens awareness? The fact that no isolated antecedent ever suffices, while totality sufficiency tracks awareness-inclusion.

Objection 13: could the Buddhist not retort that total-set causation without agent already explains Meru counterfactuals? If Meru assemblage lacks clay, wheel, water and hands, pot absence follows without Lord. Why add awareness?

Reply to Objection 13: absence of members explains which totality is absent, not why totalities have powers at all. Clay, wheel and hands do not each contain pot, nor does their mere co-presence contain bringing-forth unless co-presence is itself a functioning. Functioning is agency's signature [Rastogi 2006, via Kaul 2022]. Inert members cannot function by themselves, for of inert by itself anywhere is inability [tsa-a08-v24]. The Buddhist needs either to smuggle in functioning as primitive, which concedes agency under another name, or to dissolve functioning into further series, which launches regress. Positing awareness as functioner stops regress by grounding functioning in self-resting shining [tsa-a08-v05]. Meru therefore helps the Saiva: it shows that powers belong to assemblages, and assemblages belong to awareness.

Objection 14: does world-bodied makership not make Siva responsible for failure and evil? If the Lord alone makes pots, does the Lord alone break them? Is theodicy now unavoidable?

Reply to Objection 14: responsibility tracks freedom, not mere authorship of bodies. The Lord authors bodies as field for enjoyment-perfection, while atoms appropriate bodies through longing and action [tsa-a08-v18] [tsa-a08-v20] [tsa-a08-v21]. Breaking belongs to assemblage difference and to action-difference varying mind-nature [tsa-a08-v21]. The Lord's non-loss [tsa-a08-v07] means freedom is not diminished by permitting breakage to teach incompleteness. Impurity as incompleteness rather than non-knowledge [Acharya 2014, via Kaul 2022] explains why failure is pedagogical rather than accusatory. Pots break so that pride learns it is made [tsa-a08-v10].

## The Argument II
In constructed caused-ness the many-typed-ness toward categories' cause-effect-ness in scriptures fits, from dung worms, yogin's wish, mantra, herbs, like scorpion-arising [tsa-a08-v13]. By own-system view the Teacher promises to show the constructed [tsa-a08-v14]. The Lord full with five powers manifests power after power chiefly and stands fivefold [tsa-a08-v15]. Here the enquiry's second demand is met: the five powers must be shown to ground the tattva hierarchy. Five principles follow power-primacy, will-led Sadasiva being even-acceptance of knowing-doing [tsa-a08-v16]. Lords Siva through Ananta and five beings Sambhavas to Mantras form the pure path [tsa-a08-v17].

Objection 15: does manifold irregularity refute fixed order? If dung yields insects and mantra yields scorpions, is regularity not broken? Why call this causation rather than magic? How can dung yield insects and mantra yield scorpions yet causation remain intelligible?

Reply to Objection 15: variety is exactly what constructedness predicts, since fixed before-after appearing holds only so far, even in the more, following own nature [tsa-a08-v06]. The verse central to variety:

> evaṃ kalpite 'smin kāryatve śāstreṣu tattvānāṃ kāryakāraṇabhāvaṃ prati yat bahuprakāratvaṃ tad api saṃgataṃ gomayāt kīṭāt yogīcchāto mantrād auṣadhāt vṛścikodayavat [tsa-a08-v13]

In built causation manifold kinds among reals scripture speaks — that too fits — dung-born worms, yogin's wish, spell, herbs: as grubs to scorpions. Magic would be unintelligible exception. Multiformity is intelligible variation of fixity. Scriptural multiformity ceases to embarrass and becomes proof [tsa-a08-v13]. Each successive lower tattva draws sustenance from higher as material cause [Kaul 2018, via Kaul 2022], so dung, mantra and wish are not rival sources but different assemblages through which the same powers shine with different primacy.

Objection 16: how do five powers ground hierarchy rather than merely label it? Is power-primacy explanatory or honorific? What does will-led even-acceptance add to Sadasiva beyond a name [tsa-a08-v16]?

Reply to Objection 16: primacy explains order without making order rigid. The Lord full with five powers manifests power after power chiefly and stands fivefold [tsa-a08-v15]. Cit primacy gives Siva as resting light. Ananda primacy gives Sakti as self-enjoying fullness. Iccha primacy gives Sadasiva as will-led even-acceptance where knowing and doing are balanced [tsa-a08-v16]. Jnana primacy gives Isvara where knowing preponderates and objects clarify. Kriya primacy gives Suddhavidya where doing articulates difference without division. Lords Siva through Ananta and the five beings from Sambhavas to Mantras then articulate the pure path as subjects fit for those power-measures [tsa-a08-v17]. Hierarchy is therefore not imposed on powers. Hierarchy is powers appearing chiefly in turn. That is why lower tattvas can draw sustenance from higher as material cause [Kaul 2018, via Kaul 2022]: matter here is condensed power, not alien stuff.

Objection 17: why emit an impure path at all? If pure path already shows power-measures, is Aghoresa-Ananta emission redundant? So far directly Siva is maker, but impure path Aghoresa-Ananta emits by Lord's will for wavered atoms' enjoyment-perfection [tsa-a08-v18]. Does delegation confess incapacity?

Reply to Objection 17: delegation expresses fitness, not incapacity. Direct makership shines as pure subjects. Indirect emission through Aghoresa-Ananta fits wavered atoms whose throbbing is incomplete and other-natured [tsa-a08-v19]. The waverer is incomplete, other-natured throbbing, mere longing, future fitness for bounding, not man's impurity nor another category [tsa-a08-v19]. Passion-principle is action-bounded longing [tsa-a08-v20]. Action there is mere action; mind-nature is passion, varied by action-difference [tsa-a08-v21]. Enjoyment requires resistance. Perfection requires traversing resistance. Aghoresa emits resistance as curriculum. Abhinavagupta's treatment of causality before tattvas thus exemplifies philosophical rationalization [Allen 2003, via Kaul 2022]: ritual impurity, yogic effort and scriptural variety are grounded in power-economy, not excused by it.

## Objections and Replies
So far directly Siva is maker, but impure path Aghoresa-Ananta emits by Lord's will for wavered atoms' enjoyment-perfection [tsa-a08-v18]. The waverer is incomplete, other-natured throbbing, mere longing, future fitness for bounding, not man's impurity nor another category [tsa-a08-v19]. Passion-principle is action-bounded longing [tsa-a08-v20]. Action there is mere action; mind-nature is passion, varied by action-difference [tsa-a08-v21]. The Teacher then removes the dualist temptation at its root.

Objection 18, strongest on impurity: is impurity a second substance dualizing nondualism? If craving atoms need a separate emitter, does Siva's direct makership fail? What is impurity if not separate substance? Does inert matter stir itself?

Reply to Objection 18: impurity is from Lord's self-veiling wish, nothing else at all a thing, and by that Lord-wish-nature alone is earth-and-rest's thingness [tsa-a08-v22]:

> so 'yaṃ malaḥ parameśvarasya svātmapracchādanecchātaḥ nānyat kiṃcit vastv api ca tatparameśvarecchātmanaiva dharāder api vastutvāt [tsa-a08-v22]

Taint the Highest's veiling-scheme willed — nothing else a thing; by Highest-willed self alone earth and all things true. Impurity's stirring is by Lord-wish-force alone, for of inert by itself anywhere is inability [tsa-a08-v24]. For enjoyment-pierced atoms' perfection glorious Aghoresa emits, fittingly said [tsa-a08-v24]. Inert matter therefore never stirs itself. It stirs as veiled Lord. Dualism is avoided because veiling is self-veiling, not other-substance.

Objection 19: if impurity is mere self-veiling wish, why does it bind so hard? Existing in knowledge-kevalin facing destruction impurity does not fatten action, but yawning in dissolution-kevalin impurity-strengthened action causes transmigration-variety-enjoyment [tsa-a08-v23]. Does will-language trivialize suffering? How can wish explain transmigration variety?

Reply to Objection 19: will-language deepens rather than trivializes because will here is constitutive, not occurrent desire. The atom is mere conscious-unconscious shining, conscious form lordliness, unconscious-formedness impurity, emitting by Lord-wish-made [tsa-a08-v25]. In knowledge-kevalin impurity faces destruction and does not fatten action. In dissolution-kevalin impurity yawns and strengthened action causes transmigration-variety-enjoyment [tsa-a08-v23]. Variety of enjoyment tracks strength of veiling plus difference of action [tsa-a08-v21]. Suffering is hard because self-veiling is thorough, not because matter is strong. And it is traversable because thorough veiling is still veiling by the free one, removable by knowledge, yoga, ritual and observances [Brunner 1992, via Kaul 2022]. Inert inability secures agency against both Buddhist series and dualist matter [tsa-a08-v24].

Objection 20: does derivation of pride not excuse quietism? If potter-pride is Lord-made like pots [tsa-a08-v10], and impurity is Lord-wished [tsa-a08-v22], why strive? Is practice superfluous if binder and bound are one will?

Reply to Objection 20: derivation enjoins practice because practice is how derived agency recognizes underived agency. Abhinavagupta categorizes procedures after means [Rastogi 2012, via Kaul 2022] precisely so that each incompleteness finds its completion. Good debate is reasoning toward recognition [Matilal 1998, via Kaul 2022], not wrangling toward victory. Ritual and doctrine were already rationalized as means [Allen 2003, via Kaul 2022] [Kaul 2019, via Kaul 2022]. Quietism would follow only if Lord-made meant Lord-determined from outside. It means Lord-functioning from inside as potter, mantra-user, yogin and knower. Striving is shining recognizing resting.

## The Synthesis
Maya-principle as inert-pervading material cause grounds world-birth, while Maya-power distinct shows it forth [tsa-a08-v25]. Two-formedness extends kala-to-earth [tsa-a08-v26]. What shines in resolve exists outside too with clear form like pot [tsa-a08-v28]. Maya-kala-sky-flowers too run this course, purely affirmative reason [tsa-a08-v29]. Gurus are cited as proof in two-formedness [tsa-a08-v27]. Those enjoying direct-realization knowing with Maya-kala-nature-mind as object are perfected indeed [tsa-a08-v30]. Thus standing, from Maya-principle is world-birth [tsa-a08-v31]. Though non-sequential, by stated view sequence appears [tsa-a08-v32].

Objection 21: does extending two-formedness to sky-flowers trivialize proof? Can resolve-shining guarantee extramental existence? Who is perfected by knowing Maya-objects directly?

Reply to Objection 21: extension does not trivialize but delimits. Two-formedness means every determinate shining has inner resolve-form and outer clear-form like pot [tsa-a08-v28]. Sky-flowers share resolve-form without gaining stable outer assemblage, which is why they run this course yet fail to nourish by degrees [tsa-a08-v09] [tsa-a08-v29]. Guarantee is not claimed for every resolve. Guarantee is claimed for resolve backed by total assemblage fixed in place-time-thing-mass [tsa-a08-v09]. Gurus are cited as proof because they discern which resolves are assemblage-backed [tsa-a08-v27]. Those enjoying direct-realization with Maya-kala-nature-mind as object are perfected indeed [tsa-a08-v30] because they know objects as Maya-effects without mistaking resolve alone for world-birth [tsa-a08-v31] [tsa-a08-v37]. Affirmative reason here is purely clarificatory [tsa-a08-v29], not inflationary.

Objection 22: does sequence-appearing not reintroduce brute time? Though non-sequential, by stated view sequence appears [tsa-a08-v32]. If appearing is non-sequential in truth, is sequence illusion? How can per-self kala groups divided yet become one by Lord's wish like social selves [tsa-a08-v33]? Is unity social metaphor or ontology?

Reply to Objection 22: sequence-appearing is willed fixity, not illusion and not brute. Non-sequential shining appears sequentially when assemblages are fixed before-after only so far [tsa-a08-v06] [tsa-a08-v32]. Per-self kala groups divided yet may become one by Lord's wish like social selves [tsa-a08-v33]; pure is Lord-objected work fit for gaining nature from world-opposition [tsa-a08-v33] [tsa-a08-v34]. Social analogy is ontology because both cases exhibit many centers functioning as one totality without losing centers. Becoming thus is by power-descent sway [tsa-a08-v35]. Impure is opposite [tsa-a08-v36]. Kala from Maya yokes sleepers, swells, deforms Maya not atoms, hence Maya-effect-ness [tsa-a08-v37]. From mutual clinging man-kala are indistinguishable [tsa-a08-v38]. Time is therefore neither container nor fiction. Time is measure of fixed shining. Social oneness is neither metaphor nor merger. It is assemblage functioning as agent.

The synthesis without smoothing uncertainties can now be restated with the wall and variety teachings held together. The wall must be cited because synthesis is grounding:

> pāramārthike hi bhittisthānīye sthite rūpe sarvam idam ullikhyamānaṃ ghaṭate na anyathā ata eva sāmagryā eva kāruṇatvaṃ yuktam [tsa-a08-v08]

Variety must be cited because synthesis is freedom:

> evaṃ kalpite 'smin kāryatve śāstreṣu tattvānāṃ kāryakāraṇabhāvaṃ prati yat bahuprakāratvaṃ tad api saṃgataṃ gomayāt kīṭāt yogīcchāto mantrād auṣadhāt vṛścikodayavat [tsa-a08-v13]

Maker-location must be cited because synthesis preserves finite striving inside infinite making:

> ity evaṃ saṃvedanasvātantryasvabhāvaḥ parameśvara eva viśvabhāvaśarīro ghaṭāder nirmātā kumbhakārasaṃvidas tato 'nadhikatvāt kumbhakāraśarīrasya ca bhāvarāśimadhye nikṣepāt kathaṃ kumbhakāraśarīrasya kartṛtvābhimānaḥ iti cet parameśvarakṛta evāsau ghaṭādivat bhaviṣyati [tsa-a08-v10]

Veiling must be cited because synthesis refuses second substance:

> so 'yaṃ malaḥ parameśvarasya svātmapracchādanecchātaḥ nānyat kiṃcit vastv api ca tatparameśvarecchātmanaiva dharāder api vastutvāt [tsa-a08-v22]

And ultimate shining must be cited because synthesis begins and ends from pervasion fulfilled as agency:

> tatra pāramārthika etāvān kāryakāraṇabhāvo yad uta kartṛsvabhāvasya svatantrasya bhagavata evaṃvidhena śivādidharāntena vapuṣā svarūpabhinnena svarūpaviśrāntena ca prathanam [tsa-a08-v05]

## Conclusion
Recalled: ultimate shining and constructed fixed order were distinguished without separating them [tsa-a08-v05] [tsa-a08-v06]. The objection recalled: series without agent seemed economical [Dasgupta 2004, via Kaul 2022]. The reply recalled: even there agency does not lapse, wall grounds painting, total set names cause, potter-pride is made [tsa-a08-v07] [tsa-a08-v08] [tsa-a08-v10]. The consequence earned: the thesis long deferred can now be named. Causality is agency. Effect is agency's showing, assemblage its body, regularity its willed fixity, variety its freedom [tsa-a08-v09] [tsa-a08-v11] [tsa-a08-v13]. The Tantrasara thereby turns dependent origination from rival into restricted case of independence. Each successive lower tattva draws sustenance from higher as material cause [Kaul 2018, via Kaul 2022] because matter is power condensed through primacy [tsa-a08-v15] [tsa-a08-v16] [tsa-a08-v17]. Inert inability [tsa-a08-v24] and Meru control [tsa-a08-v12] jointly refute series self-sufficiency. Dung-worms and mantra-scorpions [tsa-a08-v13] jointly refute rigid necessity. Veiling as wish [tsa-a08-v22] jointly refutes dualism. Two-formedness to kala-earth [tsa-a08-v26] [tsa-a08-v28] [tsa-a08-v29] [tsa-a08-v30] [tsa-a08-v31] jointly secures world-birth from Maya without making Maya master [tsa-a08-v25] [tsa-a08-v37]. Trika Saivism as post-scriptural Mantramarga system [Sanderson 1988, via Kaul 2022] with vibrant Kashmirian continuity [Sanderson 2007a, via Kaul 2022] thus gains critical structure [Lawrence 2000, via Kaul 2022] through rationalization [Allen 2003, via Kaul 2022], reflection analysis [Kaul 2019, via Kaul 2022], creative reuse [Watson 2006, via Kaul 2022], and synthesis of consciousness-dynamism, Samkhya, Nyaya and pramana resources [Rastogi 2013, via Kaul 2022], distinguishing reasoning from wrangling [Matilal 1998, via Kaul 2022], ordering means [Rastogi 2012, via Kaul 2022], ordering removals [Brunner 1992, via Kaul 2022], and diagnosing incompleteness [Acharya 2014, via Kaul 2022], while effect remains functioning of potential agent [Rastogi 2006, via Kaul 2022].

Desideratum: what remains unresolved is how constructed necessity can bind without binding its binder, and what measure of fixity recognition still requires. If willed fixity binds atoms for enjoyment-perfection [tsa-a08-v18] [tsa-a08-v23] [tsa-a08-v24] [tsa-a08-v25], yet non-loss holds even there [tsa-a08-v07], then binding must be self-binding that teaches freedom by resistance. Future work must specify measures: how much place-time-thing-mass stability [tsa-a08-v09] must remain for practice to be intelligible, how much deviation [tsa-a08-v09] [tsa-a08-v13] must remain for freedom to be visible, and how power-descent sway [tsa-a08-v35] calibrates pure opposition-work [tsa-a08-v33] [tsa-a08-v34] against impure opposite [tsa-a08-v36], kala-yoking [tsa-a08-v37] and mutual clinging [tsa-a08-v38], so that sequence-appearing [tsa-a08-v32] neither hardens into fate nor dissolves into whim, and causality as agency becomes not only thesis but discipline.
