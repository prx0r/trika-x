Absent that, the honest remainder is: mixed six-reckoning evidence exists; the specific equation and translation do not. The claim should be retained only as R, with the strongest counter-reading stated: five kañcukas plus Māyā under mixed enumeration, not a proved “six sheaths” doctrine.
