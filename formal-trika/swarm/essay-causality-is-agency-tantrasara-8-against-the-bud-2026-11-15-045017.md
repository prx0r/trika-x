## Abstract
This essay follows Tantrasāra 8’s twofold analysis of cause-effect through its own terms, its Buddhist-style rival, and its reply from free awareness. Exposition distinguishes ultimate showing-forth from constructed fixed order; objection presses whether assemblage, seed-sprout regularity, and dream-pots can stand without a maker; reply invokes wall-like standing, potter-parity, and willed necessity. Consequence concerns whether regularity and manifold births require impersonal glue or rest within a single self-resting display that never lapses into mechanism while preserving difference, deviation, and place-time fixity across worlds, awaiting full demonstration below.
## Introduction
Exposition begins not with power but with pervasion. What runs through the world-born glory as mighty light counts as the common Whole, high Śiva [tsa-a08-v01]. What runs through only some differences counts as a category, as earth of glow-hardness-bulk form pervading world-eggs ruled from Kālāgni to Vīrabhadra [tsa-a08-v02]. Of these categories cause-effect-ness is shown, and it is twofold [tsa-a08-v03].

> tatra eṣāṃ tattvānāṃ kāryakāraṇabhāvo darśyate sa ca dvividhaḥ
> Draft gloss (l3): “Of these reals cause-effect is shown — and it is two.”

Objection in its strongest form grants regularity but denies a maker. Seed follows sprout, wheel and thread precede pot, dream images follow prior impressions, all by momentary dependents in fixed before-after array. No persisting knower is seen doing the work, and to add a free Lord explains nothing that dependence and assemblage cannot explain more sparingly.

Reply from the Teacher does not deny order but divides it. The ultimate is only the free agent-Lord’s showing-forth with Śiva-to-earth body, non-different in nature and resting in own nature [tsa-a08-v05]. The built is willed fixed order, and even there agency does not lapse, so the ultimate stands even unconstructed [tsa-a08-v07]. Consequence is therefore a test: whether fixed before-after shining can stand without wall-like standing, and whether assemblage weakens or strengthens makership.
## The Doctrinal Context
Exposition sets the whole within which parts gain sense. This world-born glory wombs endless varied enjoyer-enjoyed, and what pervades it as great-light-formed is like the great common, Supreme-Śiva-formed [tsa-a08-v01].

> yad idaṃ vibhavātmakaṃ bhuvanajātam uktaṃ garbhīkṛtānantavicitrabhoktṛbhogyaṃ tatra yad anugataṃ mahāprakāśarūpaṃ tat mahāsāmānyakalpaṃ paramaśivarūpam
> Draft gloss (l3): “This world-born glory, wombing endless enjoyers, enjoyed — what runs through all as mighty light: the commonest Whole, high Śiva.”

Partial pervasion defines a category, as earth pervading all world-eggs from Kālāgni to Vīrabhadra [tsa-a08-v02]. Cause-effect-ness of these categories is announced as twofold [tsa-a08-v03]. The naming is terse:

> pāramārthikaḥ sṛṣṭeś ca
> Draft gloss (l3): “The last, and the forth-sent.”

The genitive hangs, so the emission-side naming stays open; we do not smooth it into a finished emanation doctrine [tsa-a08-v04].

Objection holds that whole-part language is decoration. If earth already pervades its eggs and light already pervades all, then talk of ultimate and emitted adds two names for one dependence. Momentary particulars, each fixed in place-time, suffice for before-after talk without a Whole that shines as their body.

Reply insists the two names mark two standings, not two worlds. The ultimate is this much: the agent-natured free Lord’s showing-forth with such Śiva-to-earth-ended body, non-different and self-resting [tsa-a08-v05]. The constructed is made by the Lord’s will with necessity-breath, holding fixed before-after appearing only so far [tsa-a08-v06]. Consequence is that context is not background but ground: unless a wall-like standing holds, no drawn figure fits.
## The Argument
Exposition states the ultimate first. The free agent-Lord’s showing-forth with Śiva-to-earth body, non-different in nature and resting in own nature, is the measure of ultimate cause-effect-ness [tsa-a08-v05].

> tatra pāramārthika etāvān kāryakāraṇabhāvo yad uta kartṛsvabhāvasya svatantrasya bhagavata evaṃvidhena śivādidharāntena vapuṣā svarūpabhinnena svarūpaviśrāntena ca prathanam
> Draft gloss (l3): “Here the last cause-work is just this: the free Lord's self, agent-natured, in Śiva-to-earth flesh, same-natured, self-resting, shines.”

The constructed is second: made by the Lord’s will with necessity-breath, holding fixed before-after appearing, even in the more following own nature only so far, hence sprout from seed, yogin’s wish, dream and pots [tsa-a08-v06]. Even there non-loss of the Lord’s agency holds, so the unconstructed ultimate stands firm [tsa-a08-v07].

> tatrāpi ca parameśvarasya kartṛtvānapāya iti akalpito 'pi asau pāramārthikaḥ sthita eva
> Draft gloss (l3): “And there the Highest's act never falls away — so the unbuilt real stands true.”

For with ultimate wall-like standing form all this drawn fits, not otherwise, hence by the assemblage alone caused-ness is fitting [tsa-a08-v08].

> pāramārthike hi bhittisthānīye sthite rūpe sarvam idam ullikhyamānaṃ ghaṭate na anyathā ata eva sāmagryā eva kāruṇatvaṃ yuktam
> Draft gloss (l3): “For given the true's wall-like steadiness, all this drawn fits, else not. So by the mass alone causation befits.”

Objection at full strength says this concedes the Buddhist point. If assemblage alone causes, if seed-sprout holds only when fixed order appears and fails when a yogin’s wish intervenes, then efficacy lives in co-presence and absence, not in a Self. Wall-talk is metaphor for stable conditions; freedom-talk masks irregularity; non-difference collapses maker into made and leaves no work for Śiva to do.

Reply turns assemblage into evidence. The weave is made of all beings by free-awareness greatness, thus of fixed own place-time-thing-mass nature, each thing-nature completed being-thus, with deviation scaling effect-difference by degrees [tsa-a08-v09]. The Lord world-thing-bodied alone makes pots, since potter-awareness is nothing beyond and potter-body is placed amid things, and even maker-pride is Lord-made like pots [tsa-a08-v10]. Hence assemblage-doctrine strengthens awareness-alone’s makership, world-bodied [tsa-a08-v11]. On Meru abiding there no such pot would be, proving conditions rule kinds without ruling out the ruler [tsa-a08-v12]. Consequence is that total-set causation needs a holder: without wall-like standing no drawing holds, with it every fixed array shines as freedom’s measure.
## Objections and Replies
Exposition restates the potter parity. The free-awareness-natured Lord alone, world-thing-bodied, is maker of pots, since potter-awareness is nothing beyond and potter-body is cast amid the thing-mass [tsa-a08-v10].

> ity evaṃ saṃvedanasvātantryasvabhāvaḥ parameśvara eva viśvabhāvaśarīro ghaṭāder nirmātā kumbhakārasaṃvidas tato 'nadhikatvāt kumbhakāraśarīrasya ca bhāvarāśimadhye nikṣepāt kathaṃ kumbhakāraśarīrasya kartṛtvābhimānaḥ iti cet parameśvarakṛta evāsau ghaṭādivat bhaviṣyati
> Draft gloss (l3): “So bright-knowing-free, the Highest alone, world-stuff bodied, pots and the like he builds — for pot-mind is nothing beyond him, and the pot-body cast amid thing-masses — how is the pot-body's maker-pride? Then thus: the Highest-made even that shall be, like pots and the like.”

Objection presses hardest here. If potter-mind is nothing beyond, then either the potter makes and the Lord is redundant, or the Lord makes and moral effort, craft, and yogic discipline are illusions. To call pride Lord-made insults experience: the potter feels effort, fails, learns. A world-bodied maker explains too much and therefore nothing, dissolving finite responsibility into cosmic display.

Reply distinguishes levels without dividing being. Pride, effort, and failure belong to the placed body amid masses, while knowing belongs nowhere placed because it pervades [tsa-a08-v10]. Hence the assemblage-creed upholds world-bodied knowing’s making alone [tsa-a08-v11].

> tasmāt sāmagrīvādo 'pi viśvaśarīrasya saṃvedanasyaiva kartṛtāyām upodbalakaḥ
> Draft gloss (l3): “Hence the mass-creed too the world-bodied knowing's making alone upholds.”

Place-time fixity confirms rather than threatens this, for on Meru no such pot would be [tsa-a08-v12]. Consequence is that finite making is not denied but housed: the potter truly shapes within the mass, yet shaping as such is awareness standing fivefold and wall-like beneath every hand.
## The Synthesis
Exposition shows the constructed in own-system view [tsa-a08-v14]. The constructed is willed with necessity-breath, holding fixed before-after appearing only so far, proven by sprout from seed, yogin’s wish, dream and pots [tsa-a08-v06].

> kalpitas tu kāryakāraṇabhāvaḥ parameśecchayā niyatiprāṇayā nirmitaḥ sa ca yāvati yadā niyatapaurvāparyāvabhāsanaṃ saty api adhike svarūpānugatam etāvaty eva tena yogīcchāto 'pi aṅkuro bījād api svapnādau ghaṭāder apīti
> Draft gloss (l3): “But the built cause-work the Lord's will wove, fate-threaded: hold the fixed first-later show, even beyond, still self-true, only so — so from yogin's wish, from seed the sprout, in dream and beyond, pots and the like.”

In that constructed caused-ness manifold kinds fit, from dung worms, yogin’s wish, mantra, herbs, like scorpion-arising [tsa-a08-v13]. The weave holds fixed own place-time-thing-mass nature by free-awareness greatness, with deviation feeding effect-difference by degrees [tsa-a08-v09]. Here we keep the reading open: the draft keeps fixed, but the niyata fork is noted and left unresolved rather than smoothed [tsa-a08-v09].

Objection says variety refutes necessity. Dung-to-worm, spell-to-cure, wish-to-sprout break seed-law, dream breaks waking-law, so no single necessity threads them. Either law is exception-ridden description or will is arbitrary interruption.

Reply roots fivefold standing in powers. Full with five powers, manifesting power after power chiefly, the Lord stands fivefold [tsa-a08-v15]. Awareness-led is Śiva, bliss-led power, will-led Sadāśiva as even-acceptance of knowing-doing, knowledge-power-led Īśvara, action-power-led lore [tsa-a08-v16]. Lords Śiva through Ananta with five pervading particulars form the pure path [tsa-a08-v17]. Consequence is that variety needs will plus fixity: only a free measurer can hold many measures without breaking measure.
## Conclusion
Exposition recalls the wall without which no figure fits [tsa-a08-v08]. Objection recalled that assemblage, deviation-scaled difference, and Meru-counterfactuals seemed to work without a Self [tsa-a08-v09] [tsa-a08-v12]. Reply recalled that even there agency does not lapse and the ultimate stands even unconstructed [tsa-a08-v07].

> tatrāpi ca parameśvarasya kartṛtvānapāya iti akalpito 'pi asau pāramārthikaḥ sthita eva
> Draft gloss (l3): “And there the Highest's act never falls away — so the unbuilt real stands true.”

For the assemblage upholds world-bodied knowing’s making alone [tsa-a08-v11]. What follows, now earned and named, is the thesis: Causality Is Agency. No effect holds by bare before-after or bare mass; holding itself is shining, shining is the free Lord showing-forth as Śiva-to-earth body, non-different and self-resting [tsa-a08-v05].
