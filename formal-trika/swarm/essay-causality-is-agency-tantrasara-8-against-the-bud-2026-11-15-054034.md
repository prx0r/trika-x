## Abstract
 in its own terms begins from pervasion: what runs through the world as mighty light is taken as the common whole [tsa-a08-v01]. A category is what pervades only some differences, as earth pervades world-eggs [tsa-a08-v02]. The objection arises at once: if all is one light, why speak of cause and effect at all? Could sequence alone do the work? The reply is twofold: cause-effect-ness is shown as twofold [tsa-a08-v03], ultimate and of emission, though the second term hangs in an uncertain genitive that this essay leaves open [tsa-a08-v04]. The consequence is that freedom never lapses even within fixed order [tsa-a08-v07]. What holds assemblage-talk together? Who makes the pot? How does variety fit?
## Introduction
Exposition: the Teacher first fixes what pervades everything and what pervades only something. What runs through all as great-light-formed is like the great common, Supreme-Siva-formed [tsa-a08-v01]. By contrast, whatever form pervades only some differences is a category, as earth of glow-hardness-bulk form pervading all world-eggs ruled from Kalagni to Virabhadra [tsa-a08-v02]. Cause-effect-ness concerning these categories is then shown, and it is twofold [tsa-a08-v03].
Objection, in its strongest form: if pervasion already explains unity and difference, why add causality? Is causality not merely observed before-after regularity, needing no agent? What work does a Lord do that succession cannot do? This essay shows a preliminary mapping before answering.
The reply from the primary voice: the ultimate is just this much, namely the showing-forth of the agent-natured free Lord with Siva-to-earth body, non-different in nature and resting in own nature:

tatra pāramārthika etāvān kāryakāraṇabhāvo yad uta kartṛsvabhāvasya svatantrasya bhagavata evaṃvidhena śivādidharāntena vapuṣā svarūpabhinnena svarūpaviśrāntena ca prathanam [tsa-a08-v05]

Draft gloss (after l3): "the free Lord's self, agent-natured, in Siva-to-earth flesh, same-natured, self-resting, shines." The ultimate so defined stands even when unconstructed [tsa-a08-v07].
Consequence: if ultimate causation is self-showing, then constructed fixed order must be reassessed rather than dismissed [tsa-a08-v06]. How can mere sequence explain production? I argue that the Tantrasara reframes the question from succession to shining.
## Historical Context
Exposition: Trika Saivism emerged in the middle of the ninth century as a major post-scriptural ritual system of the Mantramarga [Sanderson 1988, via Kaul 2022]. Kashmir possessed vibrant Tantric Saiva traditions up through at least the thirteenth century [Sanderson 2007a, via Kaul 2022]. Abhinavagupta is engaged in providing critical philosophical structure to Trika scriptures [Lawrence 2000, via Kaul 2022].
Objection: was this merely ritual elaboration without philosophical stakes? Could systematization be dismissed as apologetics? What distinguishes philosophical rationalization from scriptural repetition?
Reply: Abhinavagupta's treatment of causality before tattvas exemplifies philosophical rationalization of ritual and doctrine [Allen 2003, via Kaul 2022]. He offers an analytical account of reflection before phonemic emanation on exactly parallel lines [Kaul 2019, via Kaul 2022]. Like his predecessor he was a master of creative reuse [Watson 2006, via Kaul 2022].
Consequence: the eighth ahnika of the Tantrasara must be read as intervention, not compendium. I argue that its twofold causality belongs to this program of giving reasons for scriptural variety. How did predecessors make dynamism of consciousness, Samkhya ontology, Nyaya-Vaisesika inference, and Vijnanavada pramana ideas serve nondualism [Rastogi 2013, via Kaul 2022]? That inheritance frames the Buddhist encounter.
## Literature and Method
Exposition: method here is close reading under verse protocol, quoting only packet Sanskrit verbatim with draft glosses, leaving uncertain readings open. Each successive lower tattva draws sustenance from higher tattvas as material cause [Kaul 2018, via Kaul 2022]. The concept of cause manifests fully in the Saiva notion of agent, and effect is understood as functioning of the potential agent [Rastogi 2006, via Kaul 2022].
Objection: does such a method beg the question by privileging the Saiva voice? Is debate reduced to wrangling rather than reasoning? What separates good debate based on reasoning from bad debate based on wrangling [Matilal 1998, via Kaul 2022]?
Reply: this essay shows its procedure by staging the rival at strongest, then answering from Abhinavagupta with cited verse, then drawing consequence. Abhinavagupta categorizes Trika procedures after means [Rastogi 2012, via Kaul 2022]. Earlier scriptures removed impurity by knowledge, yoga, ritual, and observances [Brunner 1992, via Kaul 2022].
Consequence: impurity as incompleteness of knowledge rather than non-knowledge must guide reading of mala passages [Acharya 2014, via Kaul 2022]. This essay shows that causality passages and mala passages belong to one argument. What holds wall and picture together if not agency?
## The Doctrinal Context
Exposition: the ultimate cause-effect-ness is the free agent-Lord's showing-forth with Siva-to-earth body, non-different and self-resting [tsa-a08-v05]. The constructed cause-effect-ness is made by the Lord's will with necessity-breath, as fixed before-after appearing, only so far even in the more [tsa-a08-v06]. Even there is non-loss of the Lord's agency, thus the unconstructed ultimate stands firm [tsa-a08-v07].
Objection: does twofoldness double the world? If ultimate already shines as all, why construct fixed order? Is constructed order redundant, or does it betray freedom?
Reply: constructed order is willed fixed appearing, proved by sprout from seed, yogin's wish, dream, and pots [tsa-a08-v06]. With ultimate wall-like standing form all this drawn fits, not otherwise, hence by assemblage alone is caused-ness fitting [tsa-a08-v08]. The assemblage is made of all-being's weave by free-awareness greatness, of fixed own place-time-thing-mass nature [tsa-a08-v09].
Consequence: deviation and degree follow assemblage difference, as effect's other-kind-ness nourishes by degrees [tsa-a08-v09]. Here the packet notes a niyata/nirata fork; this essay keeps fixed as doctrinally fitting without smoothing the philology [tsa-a08-v09]. Does agency lapse when order appears fixed?
## Encountering the Interlocutor
Exposition: the Buddhist theory of dependent origination holds that phenomena happen in series, certain phenomena being followed by others [Dasgupta 2004, via Kaul 2022]. On that view, to be a cause is to be an invariably antecedent condition, and no agent over and above the series need be posited [tsa-a08-v03].
The objection in strongest form, on behalf of the Buddhist: regularity suffices. Seed-then-sprout, wish-then-object, dream-then-pot are explained by place in a series without invoking free awareness [tsa-a08-v06]. Why posit a wall behind the painting? Is the wall not an extra metaphysical cost? How can an unconstructed shining explain why Meru conditions rule out such-and-such pot [tsa-a08-v12]? What explains invariability without reifying consciousness?
The reply from Abhinavagupta: invariability itself is only so-far shining, willed with necessity-breath [tsa-a08-v06]. For with wall-like standing ultimate form all drawn fits, not otherwise [tsa-a08-v08]. The text cited:

pāramārthike hi bhittisthānīye sthite rūpe sarvam idam ullikhyamānaṃ ghaṭate na anyathā ata eva sāmagryā eva kāruṇatvaṃ yuktam [tsa-a08-v08]

Draft gloss (after l3): "given the true's wall-like steadiness, all this drawn fits, else not. So by the mass alone causation befits." Assemblage-doctrine thus strengthens makership of awareness alone, world-bodied [tsa-a08-v11].
Consequence: series-talk is retained but grounded; total set, not isolated antecedent, is called cause [tsa-a08-v08]. Who makes the pot if not the potter?
## The Argument I
Exposition: the free-awareness-natured Supreme Lord alone, world-thing-bodied, is maker of pots and the rest, since potter-awareness is nothing beyond and potter-body is placed amid thing-mass [tsa-a08-v10]. Hence assemblage-doctrine strengthens awareness-alone's makership [tsa-a08-v11]. On Meru abiding there no such pot would be [tsa-a08-v12].
Objection: does this dissolve finite agency into divine monopoly? If potter-awareness is nothing beyond, is human effort illusory? Why preserve talk of total set if one maker does all? Could the Buddhist not retort that total-set causation without agent already explains Meru counterfactuals?
Reply: the Teacher anticipates maker-pride: how is the potter-body's maker-pride? Thus if Lord-made even that will be, like pots and the rest [tsa-a08-v10]. Full citation:

ity evaṃ saṃvedanasvātantryasvabhāvaḥ parameśvara eva viśvabhāvaśarīro ghaṭāder nirmātā kumbhakārasaṃvidas tato 'nadhikatvāt kumbhakāraśarīrasya ca bhāvarāśimadhye nikṣepāt kathaṃ kumbhakāraśarīrasya kartṛtvābhimānaḥ iti cet parameśvarakṛta evāsau ghaṭādivat bhaviṣyati [tsa-a08-v10]

Draft gloss (after l3): "bright-knowing-free, the Highest alone, world-stuff bodied, pots he builds — for pot-mind is nothing beyond him, and pot-body cast amid masses — how maker-pride? Highest-made even that shall be."
Consequence: finite pride is not denied but derived; it too is made [tsa-a08-v10]. I argue that sāmagrī is thereby converted from rival explanation into evidence for agency [tsa-a08-v11]. What confirms that assemblage-talk strengthens awareness? How does deviation scale difference?
## The Argument II
Exposition: in constructed caused-ness the many-typed-ness toward categories' cause-effect-ness in scriptures fits, from dung worms, yogin's wish, mantra, herbs, like scorpion-arising [tsa-a08-v13]. By own-system view the Teacher promises to show the constructed [tsa-a08-v14]. The Lord full with five powers manifests power after power chiefly and stands fivefold [tsa-a08-v15].
Objection: does manifold irregularity refute fixed order? If dung yields insects and mantra yields scorpions, is regularity not broken? Why call this causation rather than magic? How can dung yield insects and mantra yield scorpions yet causation remain intelligible?
Reply: variety is exactly what constructedness predicts, since fixed before-after appearing holds only so far, even in the more, following own nature [tsa-a08-v06]. The verse:

evaṃ kalpite 'smin kāryatve śāstreṣu tattvānāṃ kāryakāraṇabhāvaṃ prati yat bahuprakāratvaṃ tad api saṃgataṃ gomayāt kīṭāt yogīcchāto mantrād auṣadhāt vṛścikodayavat [tsa-a08-v13]

Draft gloss (after l3): "in built causation manifold kinds among reals scripture speaks — that too fits — dung-born worms, yogin's wish, spell, herbs: as grubs to scorpions." Five principles follow power-primacy, will-led Sadāsiva being even-acceptance of knowing-doing [tsa-a08-v16].
Consequence: scriptural multiformity ceases to embarrass and becomes proof [tsa-a08-v13]. Lords Siva through Ananta and five beings Sāmbhavas to Mantras form the pure path [tsa-a08-v17]. Why emit an impure path at all?
## Objections and Replies
Exposition: so far directly Siva is maker, but impure path Aghoresa-Ananta emits by Lord's will for wavered atoms' enjoyment-perfection [tsa-a08-v18]. The waverer is incomplete, other-natured throbbing, mere longing, future fitness for bounding, not man's impurity nor another category [tsa-a08-v19]. Passion-principle is action-bounded longing [tsa-a08-v20]. Action there is mere action; mind-nature is passion, varied by action-difference [tsa-a08-v21].
Objection, strongest: is impurity a second substance dualizing nondualism? If craving atoms need a separate emitter, does Siva's direct makership fail? What is impurity if not separate substance? Does inert matter stir itself?
Reply: impurity is from Lord's self-veiling wish, nothing else at all a thing, and by that Lord-wish-nature alone is earth-and-rest's thingness [tsa-a08-v22]:

so 'yaṃ malaḥ parameśvarasya svātmapracchādanecchātaḥ nānyat kiṃcit vastv api ca tatparameśvarecchātmanaiva dharāder api vastutvāt [tsa-a08-v22]

Draft gloss (after l3): "taint the Highest's veiling-scheme willed — nothing else a thing; by Highest-willed self alone earth and all things true." Impurity's stirring is by Lord-wish-force alone, for of inert by itself anywhere is inability [tsa-a08-v24]. For enjoyment-pierced atoms' perfection glorious Aghoresa emits, fittingly said [tsa-a08-v24].
Consequence: existing in knowledge-kevalin facing destruction impurity does not fatten action, but yawning in dissolution-kevalin impurity-strengthened action causes transmigration-variety-enjoyment [tsa-a08-v23]. The atom is mere conscious-unconscious shining, conscious form lordliness, unconscious-formedness impurity, emitting by Lord-wish-made [tsa-a08-v25]. I argue that inert inability secures agency against both Buddhist series and dualist matter [tsa-a08-v24].
## The Synthesis
Exposition: Māyā-principle as inert-pervading material cause grounds world-birth, while Māyā-power distinct shows it forth [tsa-a08-v25]. Two-formedness extends kalā-to-earth [tsa-a08-v26]. What shines in resolve exists outside too with clear form like pot [tsa-a08-v28]. Māyā-kalā-sky-flowers too run this course, purely affirmative reason [tsa-a08-v29].
Objection: does extending two-formedness to sky-flowers trivialize proof? Can resolve-shining guarantee extramental existence? Who is perfected by knowing Māyā-objects directly?
Reply: gurus are cited as proof in two-formedness [tsa-a08-v27]. Those enjoying direct-realization knowing with Māyā-kalā-nature-mind as object are perfected indeed [tsa-a08-v30]. Thus standing, from Māyā-principle is world-birth [tsa-a08-v31]. Though non-sequential, by stated view sequence appears [tsa-a08-v32].
Consequence: per-self kalā groups divided yet may become one by Lord's wish like social selves; pure is Lord-objected work fit for gaining nature from world-opposition [tsa-a08-v33] [tsa-a08-v34]. Becoming thus is by power-descent sway [tsa-a08-v35]. Impure is opposite [tsa-a08-v36]. Kalā from Māyā yokes sleepers, swells, deforms Māyā not atoms, hence Māyā-effect-ness [tsa-a08-v37]. From mutual clinging man-kalā are indistinguishable [tsa-a08-v38]. This essay shows synthesis without smoothing uncertainties.
## Conclusion
 recalled: ultimate shining and constructed fixed order were distinguished without separating them [tsa-a08-v05] [tsa-a08-v06]. The objection recalled: series without agent seemed economical [Dasgupta 2004, via Kaul 2022]. The reply recalled: even there agency does not lapse, wall grounds painting, total set names cause, potter-pride is made [tsa-a08-v07] [tsa-a08-v08] [tsa-a08-v10].
The consequence earned: the thesis long deferred can now be named. Causality is agency. Effect is agency's showing, assemblage its body, regularity its willed fixity, variety its freedom [tsa-a08-v09] [tsa-a08-v11] [tsa-a08-v13]. I argue that Tantrasara 8 thereby turns pratītyasamutpāda from rival into restricted case of svātantrya. What remains unresolved is how constructed necessity can bind without binding its binder, and what measure of fixity recognition still requires.
