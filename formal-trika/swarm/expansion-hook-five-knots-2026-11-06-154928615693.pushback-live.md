## OBJECTION

The claim is an R reconstruction at `tsa-a08-v55`/`tsa-a08-v56`, and both locators have **no formal entry** in the bank. `CLM-001` through `CLM-004` likewise have **no formal entry**. So every cited proposition is suspect until banked evidence supports it; here it does not. R cannot promote itself by repeating its own gloss.

The `tantrasara-a08:tsa08-p03` bundle does not rescue the claim. At most it records a six-kañcuka reckoning with **five + māyā** and explicitly notes “mixed reckonings recorded”; `maya_six_count` is **MODEL / CONTEXT_ONLY** and only says 8.55 describes six coverings starting with māyā, “this checks the candidate list.” `key_five_kancukas` gives “names” with T1 frequency **PREREGISTERED-ONLY**, not a finding. `pairings_distinct` itself flags its pairing gloss as R. None of this establishes the exact Sanskrit line, the identity “Māyā-six = six sheaths named,” or the verse’s wording about awareness set aside and kalā standing “like sheaths.”

Strongest counter-reading: `māyādiṣaṭka` is a **count of six coverings beginning with māyā**—māyā plus five kañcukas—not a title “Māyā-six” for a named set of six sheaths. On that reading, “kañcukaṣaṭkam” is a mixed reckoning/gloss, and “sheath-like” is a simile for kalā and the rest, not a definitional identity. The bank’s own `count_5` and `maya_six_count` support that counter-reading at least as well as the claim.
