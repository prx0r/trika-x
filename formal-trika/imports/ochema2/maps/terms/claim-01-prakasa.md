# CLAIM: "Indeed, this Light of consciousness shines at all times; so, as it cannot be denied, what is the use of applying contrived means of knowledge to it? All that needs be done is to recognise that the One Light exists and that it illumines the manifestation of all things."

STATUS: T (textual)
LABEL: established (as a textual claim) / the metaphysical reading is speculative

SOURCE:
  Text: Tantrāloka 1.54
  Translation: Dyczkowski, Vol 1
  Sanskrit: prakāśo nāma yaś cāyaṁ sarvadaiva prakāśate | anapahnavanīyatvāt kiṁ tasmin mānakalpanaiḥ ||

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Manifestation has transcendental priority. Nothing enters an account
  of reality except through appearing — even denial occurs as a
  manifest cognition. The light of consciousness is "proved from the
  start" (ādisiddha): any proof already presupposes it.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: 1 — Manifestation (jñāna/prakāśa)
  Scientific terms: perception, state estimation, registration
  Alignment: science accommodates "perception is prior to any account
    of perception" as an epistemic structure; it is silent on whether
    manifestation is "transcendentally prior" to everything.

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — the epistemological claim (no account of reality except
  through appearing) is checkable as philosophy of perception. The
  metaphysical reading (the Light is one consciousness) is the
  speculative layer, labelled as such.

# CLAIM: "Supreme consciousness itself... is always by its very nature essentially the aesthetic relish of consciousness which is independent of all things and most free."

STATUS: T (textual) — PTv p.19, cited in TĀ 1 commentary (attribution corrected in rasarasa.md)
LABEL: interpretive (the identity of consciousness with relish is the tradition's claim; the scientific link is the rasa hypothesis)

SOURCE:
  Text: Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa p.19, cited in TĀ 1
  Translation: Dyczkowski

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Consciousness is not merely aware — it IS aesthetic relish
  (camatkāra). Feeling is not added to awareness; it is what
  awareness is. This is the strongest textual statement of the rasa
  doctrine.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: 6 — Value weighting (rāga) + the rasa hypothesis cell
  Scientific terms: valence, affect, the geometry-of-quality
    hypothesis (QRI symmetry theory, Tymoczko)
  Alignment: science accommodates "affective valence is intrinsic to
    some states" (Panksepp); it does not establish "consciousness IS
    relish" — that is the tradition's claim (T) and the programme's
    testable hypothesis (H), not an established identity.

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — the rasa hypothesis is the most testable lead in the
  programme: does felt quality track the geometry of state space?
  (Chordonomicon / DEAM datasets — see frontiers/04-rasa-hypothesis.md)
