# CLAIM: "The universe... is the condensation of the flood of the juice (rasa) of consciousness, that has assumed a (fixed defined) form."

STATUS: T (textual) — direct TĀ root verse (TĀ 5, line 4146, validated in rasarasa.md)
LABEL: speculative (the metaphysical claim) / interpretive (the mapping)

SOURCE:
  Text: Tantrāloka 5 (opening invocation), Vol 5 line 4146
  Translation: Dyczkowski — "Bhairava's visible body as the
    universe, which is the condensation of the flood of the juice
    (rasa) of consciousness, that has assumed a (fixed defined)
    form."

WHAT IT SAYS:
  The physical world is not the opposite of consciousness — it is
  consciousness's own aesthetic "juice" thickened into form. This
  is the strongest textual statement of the rasa-ontology link:
  matter as condensed experience.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: A (Ground) + 6 (Value weighting)
  Scientific terms: none direct. The closest existing concepts:
    the intrinsic-nature question (Russellian monism: physical
    descriptions are structural; the intrinsic nature is
    unknown), and the rasa hypothesis (quality = geometry).
  Alignment: science is SILENT — no established result either way.
    This is a metaphysical claim about the nature of matter, not a
    testable scientific claim in its current form.

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Only via the testable shadow: IF the rasa hypothesis (quality
  corresponds to geometry) is established for experience, THEN the
  question "does the geometry of physical states have a quality
  side?" becomes researchable in principle (Russellian monism's
  programme). The claim itself is speculative, labelled as such in
  the first sentence.

# CLAIM: "These goddesses are hypostases of the energies or rays of the light of one's own conscious nature... aspects of its ecstatic nature or 'wonder' (ciccamatkāra)."

STATUS: T (textual) — from the MVV (Mālinīvijayavārtika) 4/6cd via Dyczkowski's commentary (attribution corrected in rasarasa.md)
LABEL: interpretive

SOURCE:
  Text: TĀ 3 context; MVV 4/6cd; Dyczkowski 1992a: 202-203
  Translation: Dyczkowski

WHAT IT SAYS:
  The senses are "ecstatic wonder" (ciccamatkāra) — each modality
  is a flavour of consciousness's self-delight. Seeing = visual
  wonder; hearing = auditory wonder.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: 1 (Manifestation) + 6 (Value weighting)
  Scientific terms: modality-specific phenomenology; sensory
    quality; the valence dimension of each sensory modality
  Alignment: science accommodates "each sensory modality has its
    own qualitative character" (established); it is silent on
    whether that character is "consciousness's self-delight"
    (the tradition's reading).

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes, in the shadow form: modality-specific valence is measurable
  (the valence literature distinguishes modalities). Whether
  modality quality reduces to one "relish" dimension is the rasa
  hypothesis's question, in the sensory register.
