# CLAIM: "I-shaped consciousness is eternally self-manifesting." (ahaṃrūpā tu saṃvittirnityā svaprathanātmikā)

STATUS: T (textual)
LABEL: interpretive

SOURCE:
  Text: Tantrāloka 1.127
  Translation: Dyczkowski

WHAT IT SAYS:
  The "I" is not an ego added to consciousness — it is
  consciousness's own form. Self-manifesting "I-ness" is the
  intrinsic shape of awareness, not a construct.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: 1 (Manifestation) + the reflexivity cell
  Scientific terms: minimal self-affection, for-me-ness, the
    reflexivism/anonymism debate in contemporary philosophy
  Alignment: science and philosophy of mind debate whether
    for-me-ness is intrinsic (reflexivism) or constructed
    (anonymism). The tradition's claim aligns with reflexivism —
    a live position, not an established result.

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — the reflexivism/anonymism debate is active and has
  experimental pressure points (see frontier doc Track B §1:
  does minimal for-me-ness survive major content changes?). The
  tradition's argument for intrinsic I-ness is a philosophical
  contribution to that debate.

# CLAIM: "The Light of consciousness possesses the power to be free (svātantrya) to be as it is, both transcendent and immanent... In epistemological terms this ontic power is called vimarśa."

STATUS: T (textual)
LABEL: interpretive (the identity of freedom with awareness is the tradition's claim)

SOURCE:
  Text: TĀ 1, lines 1740-1750 (Dyczkowski's commentary on svātantrya)
  Translation: Dyczkowski

WHAT IT SAYS:
  Freedom (svātantrya) and reflective awareness (vimarśa) are the
  same power: to be aware IS to be free IS to articulate. Three
  names for one capacity of consciousness to be what it is without
  external constraint.

MAPPING:
  Grid cell: 5 (Counterfactual generation / vikalpa) + the freedom
    cell
  Scientific terms: generative models, active inference,
    counterfactual simulation — the "generation of novel
    configurations not supplied by sensory input"
  Alignment: science accommodates "agents generate novel
    counterfactuals" (generative models); it is silent on whether
    this generation is "freedom" in the metaphysical sense. The
    identification of imagination with experienced freedom is the
    tradition's claim, with the strongest textual anchor
    (ĪPK I.6.10: the imagination that moves according to its own
    will).

ROOM FOR RESEARCH:
  Yes — the claim "imagination is privileged evidence for
  spontaneous generation" is empirically approachable (imagination
  generates genuinely novel configurations — established). The
  further claim (this IS metaphysical freedom) is the speculative
  layer, labelled.
