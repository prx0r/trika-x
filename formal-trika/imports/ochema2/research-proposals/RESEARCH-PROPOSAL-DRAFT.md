# RESEARCH PROPOSAL — ochema2 (draft)

## Title

**The Tantrāloka and the Science of Experience: A Comparative
Research Programme in the Philosophy of Consciousness**

## The question

The Tantrāloka of Abhinavagupta (c. 1000 CE) contains one of the most
developed accounts of subjective experience ever written — a
functional anatomy of perception, memory, determination, imagination,
valuation, and embodiment, integrated with a metaphysics of
manifestation. What is it actually claiming? Can its claims be
expressed in the terms of contemporary cognitive science and biology
without distortion? And where does the comparison reveal genuine open
questions that neither the tradition nor the science has settled?

## Why now

Three independent lines of work have matured in parallel:

1. **Textual:** the complete Dyczkowski translation is now available
   (11 volumes, 2023), and the systematic extraction of its claims is
   substantially underway (TANTRALOKA_MASTER, the 32 findings, the
   rasa source-audit).
2. **Scientific:** developmental bioelectricity (Levin) has
   experimentally established that goal-directed, memory-bearing
   pattern control operates at the level of cell collectives —
   matching, in broad structure, what the tradition attributes to
   the body's "vital" organization.
3. **Philosophical:** the frontier enquiry (three summits, ten
   premises, three master gaps) has established precisely where the
   comparison breaks down: no established relation between
   integration, reflexivity, continuity, or ownership has been shown
   sufficient for phenomenal co-presence.

The programme's contribution is to do the mapping discipline that
none of these lines has done alone: claim by claim, status by status,
with the silos broken and the gaps named.

## Method

1. **Claim files.** Every claim from the primary source, in the
   source's words, with location, plain-language statement, mapping
   onto the nine-operation grid (ĪPK 1.3.7), and a status
   (textual / reconstructed / empirical / correspondence /
   hypothesis / rejected).
2. **Comparison grid.** The same grid applied to Neoplatonism,
   Buddhism, and the contemplative traditions, so that correspondences
   are recorded without merging vocabularies.
3. **Alignment check.** Each mapped claim tested against published
   science: accommodated, contradicted, or silent.
4. **Frontier files.** The open questions, each with what research
   would move it.
5. **Honesty rule.** No shared vocabulary counts as convergence
   without a shared constraint or prediction. Speculative claims are
   labelled speculative in the first sentence.

## The deliverables

1. **The claim-by-claim map of the Tantrāloka** — a standardised,
   source-anchored, status-labelled inventory of what the text
   claims about reality (the first 32 from the COMPENDIUM, then the
   āhnika-level map).
2. **The comparison map** — Neoplatonism, Buddhism, the contemplative
   traditions on the same grid.
3. **The alignment report** — which claims are accommodated by
   current science, which are contradicted, which are silently open.
4. **The frontier dossier** — the master gaps, the two identity
   hypotheses, and the research designs that would move them.
5. **One empirical test** — the rasa hypothesis (felt quality vs.
   geometry of state space) checked against the Chordonomicon /
   DEAM datasets.

## The testable claims

1. **Rasa hypothesis (H):** felt quality tracks the geometry of the
   relevant state space. Test: Chordonomicon (666k songs) — valence
   annotations vs. geometric distance in Tymoczko's chord space.
   Prediction: songs in the same chord-region share annotated
   valence beyond conventional music-theoretic baselines. Rival:
   conventional music theory predicts the same variance → the
   hypothesis must add predictive power to survive.
2. **Significance = valence? (H):** intrinsic biological significance
   (Kolchinsky & Wolpert) constitutes felt valence. Test: designed
   contrasts between significance-bearing and merely-informative
   states, using the valence literature's instruments (DEAM,
   affective neuroscience).
3. **Closure = co-presence? (H):** objective causal closure
   constitutes phenomenal co-presence. Test: Levin's cognitive light
   cone — does goal scale track phenomenal unity better than rival
   boundary criteria (Markov blanket, Φ-max, anatomical)?

## What the programme is NOT

- Not a theory of consciousness. It posits nothing.
- Not a proof of Śaiva metaphysics. The tradition's claims are
  graded like any other source.
- Not a unification project. Mapping is mapping; the ≅ symbol is
  retired.
- Not a bridge-builder by default. Most correspondences will be
  recorded as correspondences and left there.

## The one question everything reduces to

> Does intrinsic biological self-concern constitute phenomenal
> presence, or merely accompany it?

The programme does not answer this. It maps the terrain around it,
claim by claim, status by status — which is the only way the answer,
if it exists, can be constructed from what is already established.
