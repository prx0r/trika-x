## WHAT EACH FRAMEWORK ACTUALLY PROVES

### 1. FULLER — SYNERTETICS

**Proven:**

| Claim | Evidence | Limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Tetrahedron is the minimum spatial enclosure in 3D (4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 faces) | Euler's topology + geometric proof | Does not prove phenomenal interiority |
| Tetrahedron is the only omnisymmetrical system that can inside-out | Geometric demonstration (Syn 232.01) | Kinematic, not dynamical |
| Tetravolume accounting gives rational volumes for all Platonic forms | Mathematical derivation (Syn 000.127) | Alternative coordinate system, not a replacement for standard physics |
| IVM is the densest sphere-packing lattice | Close-packing theorem | Describes a coordinate system, not the physical universe |
| Synergy: whole-system behaviour unpredictable from parts | Demonstrated (Syn 108.03) | Demonstration, not a formal measure |
| Tensegrity: continuous tension, discontinuous compression | Structural principle (Syn 700.01) | Descriptive, not predictive |
| Pattern integrity: identity persists through material change | Generalized/special-case distinction (Syn 905.42) | Does not entail phenomenal persistence |
| Jitterbug: VE → icosahedron → octahedron → tetrahedron → zero | Kinematic model | Requires operator; not a dynamical law |

**Unproven:**

| Claim | Problem |
|---|---|
| Nature's coordinate system IS tetrahedral | Competing coordinate systems also work |
| T/E module ratio explains electron/proton mass | Numerical coincidence, no causal derivation |
| The tetrahedron IS the unified field | Claim without mechanism |

## THE DEFENSIBLE METAPHYSICS — FORMAL STATEMENT

### Core Identity

```
Int(Pᵢ*) = Dᵢ
```

Where:
- Pᵢ* is a causally closed, self-maintaining, valenced, temporally integrated process
- Dᵢ is the intrinsic phenomenal field of that process

### The Phenomenal Tuple

```
Dᵢ = ⟨Mᵢ, ρᵢ, ∂ᵢ, Uᵢ, τᵢ, 𝒢ᵢ, νᵢ⟩
```

| Variable | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mᵢ | Manifest presence | Fuller (volume), Ñāṇavīra (the 4 symbols), Abhinavagupta (prakāśa) |
| ρᵢ | Basal self-affection | Abhinavagupta (camatkāra/rasa) |
| ∂ᵢ | Field boundary | Abhinavagupta (dehabhedāt), Levin (coupling scale) |
| Uᵢ | Unity/binding | Ñāṇavīra (the three interchanges), Tymoczko (overdetermination) |
| τᵢ | Temporal dynamics | Ñāṇavīra (accelerating moments), Fuller (jitterbug) |
| 𝒢ᵢ | Relational structure | Fuller (IVM), Tymoczko (chord space) |
| νᵢ | Local valence | Abhinavagupta (rasa), QRI (STV hypothesis) |

### Local Valence Equation (Hypothesis)

```
νᵢ(t) = Fᵢ(𝒢ᵢ(t), Rᵢ(t), ΔCᵢ(t), Eᵢ(t), Hᵢ, Ξᵢ(t))
```

Where R = regulatory condition, ΔC = error reduction, E = expectation, H = history, Ξ = context.

**ρᵢ ≠ νᵢ**: Basal self-affection is NOT local valence. ρᵢ > 0 even when νᵢ < 0.

## THE REMAINING GAPS

### Gap 1: Λₚ — The Law of Perspectival Partition

**The problem:** Why is THIS tetrahedron THIS subject and not another?

**Candidates:**
- Dehabhedāt (body difference) — descriptive, not derivable
- Topological pockets — speculative, not detected
- Rāga-niyati cycle — describes reinforcement, not origin
- Coupling scale (Levin) — empirically established for agency, not for phenomenality

**Status:** OPEN. No framework supplies a derivation.

### Gap 2: The Hard Problem

**The problem:** Why does THIS 4-term structure feel like something?

**Candidates:**
- Rasa identity (Abhinavagupta): consciousness IS self-affective manifestation
- Panpsychism (QRI): consciousness is fundamental
- Emergence: not addressed by any framework

**Status:** OPEN. The rasa identity is the most specific candidate but remains an assertion, not a derivation.

### Gap 3: The Mapping from 𝒮 to 𝒫

**The problem:** In what sense does the structure of a stimulus equal the structure of its experience?

**Candidates:**
- Tymoczko's four-space distinction (𝒮, 𝒩, 𝒫, ℛ) — identifies the gap but doesn't bridge it
- QRI's STV — hypothesises an identity but doesn't prove it
- The vīṇa passage — describes absorption but doesn't derive it

**Status:** OPEN. Tymoczko explicitly leaves this to perceptual psychology. QRI assumes an identity without proof.

## THE CONVERGENCE THAT SURVIVES

Despite all gaps, one convergence survives critical assessment across all frameworks:

> **A subject requires a minimum of 4 distinct, fully interconnected functional poles. Fewer than 4 cannot generate an inside/outside distinction. The relations between these poles are irreducible to any subset of them. The closure of these relations generates an interior that has no location in the system but is nevertheless real.**

This is the defensible metaphysics:
- 4 = minimum (Fuller, Ñāṇavīra, Levin, Tymoczko independently)
- Irreducible relations = synergy (Fuller, Levin)
- Pattern persists through material change = tensegrity/pattern integrity (Fuller, Levin)
- The interior = the subject (Abhinavagupta: ahaṅcamatkāra, Ñāṇavīra: the hierarchy of awareness)

What remains unproven: that this interior feels like something. That's the rasa hypothesis — the strongest unproven claim in the set.
