# OCHEMA 2 — THE CRYSTALLISED RESEARCH PROGRAMME

**Version 1.0 — 2026-08-04.** The founding document of ochema2.
Replaces the unified-framework ambitions. Built from the frontier docs,
the aperture model, inexternalism, the Tantrāloka extraction, the
confrontation records, and the hard lessons of the 2026-08-04
peer-review.

---

## 1. WHAT THIS PROGRAMME IS

**One sentence:** A razor-sharp comparative-philosophy research
programme that asks what the old traditions — the Tantrāloka above
all — are actually saying about the nature of reality, maps their
claims in a standardised way into the terms of existing physical and
biological theory, and identifies where genuine room for further
research exists — without positing new claims.

**Three questions, in order:**

1. **What is the Tantrāloka saying?** — primary source work, quote
   first, translation checked, scholarship consulted.
2. **Can we map it?** — can the claims be expressed in a standardised
   way, in alignment with existing physical and biological theory,
   without inventing new vocabulary or new laws?
3. **Is there room for research?** — where do the mapped claims point
   to open questions that existing science genuinely has not
   settled?

The programme does NOT ask: "does this prove a unified theory of
consciousness?" That question produced the overclaims that the
peer-review correctly destroyed. It is retired.

## 2. THE CORPUS — WHAT WE WORK ON

### 2.1 The primary source

**The Tantrāloka of Abhinavagupta** (c. 1000 CE), Dyczkowski
translation, all 11 volumes. This is the centre of gravity. Every
claim studied here first.

Companion texts (all in the corpus):
- Īśvarapratyabhijñā (Utpaladeva) + Kārikā/Vṛtti — the functional
  anatomy (nine operations)
- Spandakārikā — the pulse doctrine
- Parātrīśikā-vivaraṇa — camatkāra, the sixteen meanings of Anuttara
- Vijñāna-bhairava — practice
- The Siddhitrayī texts (Ajadapramātṛsiddhi, Sambandhasiddhi,
  Īśvarasiddhi) — the universalisation arguments, and where they fail

### 2.2 The comparison traditions

Neoplatonism (Proclus, Plotinus, Iamblichus), Buddhism (Ñāṇavīra,
Ñāṇananda), the contemplative traditions (Tibetan), and the modern
"synthetic" materials (Law of One, Seth, Cassiopaeans — held strictly
as hypothesis-generators and cultural data, never as evidence).

### 2.3 The scientific side

The published literature: Levin's bioelectric work, predictive
processing, IIT, global workspace, active inference, QRI's valence
programme, Tymoczko's music geometry, Markov blankets, real-pattern
ontology. Used as the *language* for mapping — not as a theory to be
proved.

## 3. THE METHOD — HOW WE WORK

### 3.1 The status system (from the frontier docs, kept as-is)

Every claim in every document gets a status:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **T** | Textual — explicitly stated in the source (quote + location) |
| **R** | Reconstructed — the strongest argument recoverable from the source |
| **E** | Empirical — experimentally supported |
| **C** | Correspondence — two systems address the same structural problem |
| **H** | Hypothesis — possible but unproved |
| **X** | Rejected — contradicted or no longer required |

**No tradition may inherit another tradition's conclusion merely
because they share a structure.**

### 3.2 The honesty rule (adopted after the peer-review)

> **No shared vocabulary counts as convergence without a shared
> constraint or prediction.**

Mapping a claim into scientific terms is description, not evidence.
A correspondence earns evidential weight only when it produces
something one side did not already have.

### 3.3 The grading rule (simplified, per the user's instruction)

**Most of what we work on is speculative. We say so.**

- What is **established** (peer-reviewed science, verified text) is
  marked established.
- What is **interpretive** (mapping, comparison, correspondence) is
  marked interpretive.
- What is **speculative** (the metaphysical extensions, the density
  project's ontological claims, the channeled material) is marked
  speculative — and that is not a defect, it is a label.
- No A–E gymnastics. Three labels: **established / interpretive /
  speculative.** Where a claim is speculative, it says so in the
  first sentence.

### 3.4 The unit of work

**The claim file.** For each claim studied:

```
CLAIM:        [the claim, in the source's words — T or R]
SOURCE:       [text, location, translation]
WHAT IT SAYS: [plain-language statement, checked against scholarship]
MAPPING:      [expression in existing scientific terms, if one exists]
STATUS:       [established / interpretive / speculative]
ROOM:         [does this point to an open research question? yes/no + what]
```

### 3.5 The standardised map

The mapping vocabulary is fixed, and it is **the aperture model's
nine operations** — because those are textually established in the
ĪPK and have scientific counterparts. No new terms. The map:

| Operation | Sanskrit | Function | Scientific counterpart (for mapping only) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Manifestation | jñāna/prakāśa | appearing | perception, state estimation |
| Retention | smṛti | past remains available | memory, attractors |
| Determination | apohana | this rather than that | selection, exclusion |
| Stream integration | anusandhāna | temporal/personal connection | integration, ΦID |
| Counterfactual generation | vikalpa | novel forms | generative models |
| Value weighting | rāga | what matters | semantic information, homeostasis |
| Finite possibility space | kañcukas | what is possible | embodiment, constraints |
| Cross-stream boundary | deha-bheda | where synthesis stops | bioelectric closure |
| Inter-stream influence | pratibimba | other fields affect mine | social coupling |

Every tradition's claims are mapped onto this grid (or explicitly
marked "no mapping found"). This is what breaks the silos: the
Tantrāloka's prāṇa, Iamblichus's luminous body, the Tibetan winds,
and Levin's bioelectricity can be *talked about together* — each
labeled with its status — without any of them being asserted as
identical to any other.

## 4. THE CRYSTALLISED FRONTIER (what we already established)

The frontier docs reached these conclusions. They stand.

### 4.1 The equation of the enquiry

```
Dᵢ = Λₚ(M, Πᵢ, Σᵢ)
```

- M: what makes manifestation possible at all (Sanskrit summit)
- Πᵢ: participant-specific structure (Greek summit)
- Σᵢ: concrete biological realization (scientific summit)
- Dᵢ: one private phenomenal domain
- Λₚ: the law connecting them — **still unknown**

Each summit explains one term. None explains Λₚ. This is not a
failure; it is the mapped problem-space, and it is complete.

### 4.2 What is established (and stays)

1. **Manifestness cannot be replaced by inert structure.** S(x) ⟹̸
   M(x). Utpaladeva's regress argument. (T + philosophical result)
2. **The functional anatomy is complete.** Nine operations, textual +
   scientific counterparts. (T + E)
3. **Three partition mechanisms in the Tantrāloka:** dehabhedāt (body
   differences), rāga-niyati (craving + fixation), prāṇa (the
   descent bridge). (T)
4. **The ten-premise proof of the frontier:** simultaneity, causation,
   integration, reflexivity, continuity, ownership, shared ground —
   none ⟹ co-presence. (R — rigorous, survives)
5. **Participation is the strongest model of plurality:** shared
   ground + real distinction without numerical collapse. (R/C)
6. **Two testable identity hypotheses survive:**
   - Does intrinsic biological significance = felt valence?
   - Does objective causal closure = phenomenal co-presence?
7. **Recognition is subtractive:** it changes self-positioning, not
   content. (T + testable prediction)

### 4.3 What the unified-framework detour taught us (kept as lessons, not claims)

- The ≅ symbol was never earned. Mapping ≠ isomorphism.
- The finite-dim modular-flow "defeat of CIMC" was false.
- The "eight levels of recovery" was self-coded, not independent.
- The water shield had no calculation.
- The channeled corpus is never evidence.
- **But:** the confrontations are not wasted — they are the record of
  what each theory actually claims, graded against the corpus. The
  density project is not wasted — it is a mapping exercise (what the
  traditions say about transformation across scales), and it stays a
  mapping exercise. The what-the-heck-is series stands — these are
  phenomena we do not understand, and the series documents the
  claimants' theories precisely.

## 5. THE FOUR LINES OF WORK (the roadmap)

### Line 1 — The Tantrāloka claim-by-claim map (primary)

The systematic extraction of the Tantrāloka's claims about the nature
of reality, each in a claim file with the standardised map. The
TANTRALOKA_MASTER extraction is the starting inventory. The 32
findings from the COMPENDIUM are the first 32 claim files.

### Line 2 — The comparison map (traditions)

The same grid applied to Neoplatonism, Buddhism, the contemplative
traditions. Where a tradition's claim maps onto the same grid cell,
that is recorded as a correspondence (C) — nothing more.

### Line 3 — The scientific mapping (alignment check)

For each mapped claim: does existing physical/biological theory
accommodate it, contradict it, or say nothing? This is where the
alignment requirement lives. Levin's bioelectricity, predictive
processing, valence research, music geometry — as the language, not
the theory to be proved.

### Line 4 — The open questions (frontiers)

Where the mapped claims point to questions science has not settled.
The master gaps from the frontier docs (individuation, co-presence,
manifest-value) are the starting list. Each gets a frontier file:
what is established, what is interpretive, what is speculative, and
what research would move it.

## 7. STANDARDS OF CONDUCT (binding)

1. Quote before interpreting. Interpretation is labelled
   interpretation.
2. Map in the standardised grid or say "no mapping found."
3. Three labels: established / interpretive / speculative. Say which.
4. No new terminology. The nine operations are the vocabulary.
5. A correspondence is a correspondence (C) — never a convergence,
   never an identity, unless it produces a shared constraint or
   prediction.
6. Errors are corrected in place, on record, versioned. An error
   found is a successful use of the method.
7. The programme's claims are the least of the three voices: the
   text's, the science's, and its own.
