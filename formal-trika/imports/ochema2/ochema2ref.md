### The key finding

**The Tantrāloka pipeline has ~600+ render scripts** (233 goldrender +
87 moderngl + 22 platinum + 380 clean queue) and **~380 actual
videos** (238 in tantraloka alone) — but they're fragmented: 6-second
per-verse clips, scene fragments, and title cards. The only *complete
films* with unique visuals are the 2 MOTHERFUCKER long-form films.

The video strategy: the render scripts are the raw material library;
the film-packs (corbin, stones) are the proof that the pipeline CAN
produce unique long-form films; the 50-essay companion's visual
grammar is the design system for making the next ones GOOD from the
start (per-essay visual programs, not templates).
