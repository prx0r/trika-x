## THE TEN PREMISES (the proof of the frontier)

These are the sharpest result the frontier docs produced. Each premise
is a relation that does NOT suffice for phenomenal co-presence:

1. simultaneity ⟹̸ co-presence
2. causal coupling ⟹̸ co-presence
3. information integration ⟹̸ co-presence
4. shared ground ⟹̸ shared field
5. reflexive manifestation ⟹̸ co-presence
6. continuity ⟹̸ one persisting subject
7. ownership ⟹̸ fundamental subjecthood
8. (plus: inclusion + exclusion are exhibited by every phenomenal
   domain — the only positive structural result)

**The frontier, stated exactly:**

> What makes experience occur together here, as this world, through
> this centre — and nowhere else?

This is not a vague hard problem. It is a precise missing law: the law
of phenomenal domain formation.

## THE THREE MASTER GAPS

### Gap 1 — The Individuation Gap

```
E_contentᵢ ⟹̸ E_subject
```

Determining one content is not the same operation as determining
which phenomenal field it belongs to. ĪPK 1.3.7 (apohana determines
content) + TĀ 16.242 (anusandhāna stops at the body) establish the
parts; neither establishes the identity of the operations.

**Research room:** What distinguishes content-exclusion from
field-exclusion? Is there an empirical discriminator between them?

### Gap 2 — The Co-Presence Gap

```
Integration(x,y) ⟹̸ CoPresent(x,y)
```

Science identifies systems that are causally integrated, semantically
autonomous, self-modelling, globally coherent. It cannot establish
that x and y are experienced together rather than merely processed
together. (ΦID reductions under anaesthesia correlate — they do not
establish co-presence.)

**Research room:** Is there a measurable signature of co-presence
distinct from integration? (The two identity hypotheses below are the
candidates.)

### Gap 3 — The Manifest-Value Gap

```
(Semᵢ, Vᵢ) ⟹̸ νᵢ
```

Objective significance (Kolchinsky & Wolpert: information causally
necessary for persistence) does not entail felt valence. Panksepp
establishes primary-process affect exists. Neither establishes that
either is felt.

**Research room:** The rasa hypothesis (below) is the candidate bridge
— and it is the most testable thing in the programme.

## THE TWO IDENTITY HYPOTHESES (the testable survivors)

Both are hypotheses (H). Both are stated so they can be tested. These
are the only places where the programme itself ventures a claim, and
they are the claims everything else reduces to.

### Hypothesis A — significance = valence?

> Does intrinsic biological significance constitute felt valence, or
> merely accompany it?

**Candidates for a test:** QRI's valence programme (symmetry theory),
the Chordonomicon dataset (666k songs: does felt quality track
geometric distance in Tymoczko's space?), the DEAM dataset (music
valence annotations).

### Hypothesis B — closure = co-presence?

> Does objective causal closure constitute phenomenal co-presence, or
> merely accompany it?

**Candidates for a test:** Levin's cognitive light cone (does goal
scale track phenomenal unity?), the aperture's boundary criteria vs.
rival criteria (Markov blanket, Φ-max, anatomical) — which predicts
unity/ownership/valence better?

## THE RASA HYPOTHESIS (the strongest testable lead)

**SPECULATIVE — labelled in the first sentence.**

Three frameworks independently propose that felt quality corresponds
to the geometric structure of a state space:

- Tantrāloka: camatkāra (consciousness IS aesthetic relish) — T
- QRI: symmetry theory of valence — H
- Tymoczko: chord geometry predicts felt consonance — E (the
  geometry is established; the felt-quality link is the hypothesis)

**The test:** Chordonomicon (666k songs) — does emotional quality
annotated in the data map to geometric distance in the 6D chord
space? The prediction is specific: songs with the same chord-region
should be annotated with similar valence, beyond what conventional
music theory predicts.

**What the test decides:** If yes — the rasa hypothesis earns its
first empirical anchor. If no — the hypothesis dies cleanly, which is
also a result. Either way, the programme has produced one claim that
passes the honesty rule (a shared prediction checked against data).
