## The one-sentence version

**The state you carry shapes what can happen to you next — there is real mathematics about states generating their own dynamics, and a real experiment about biological states shaping outcomes; what the traditions say beyond that is interpretation, graded accordingly.**

---

## The problem

Every tradition says something like it: you attract what you are; you
only meet what you can bear. The intuition is universal — and that's
the problem. Universal intuitions are usually either deep truths or
cheap comfort, and nothing in them distinguishes the two.

So the question: is "the state at threshold determines the outcome"
a metaphor with a soothing ring, or a claim with a mechanism?

## What is established (the sober core)

Two results, each graded on its own:

1. **Thermal time (Connes–Rovelli).** A faithful state ω of an
   operator algebra generates a flow σ_t^ω (A — theorem). Whether
   that flow IS physical time in a background-independent theory is
   the thermal-time *hypothesis* (C) — Connes and Rovelli state it
   as a hypothesis, not a theorem. Whether it is *experienced* time
   is a conjecture (C/D), not established.

2. **Quantum Bayes (Tegmark).** Observation updates a density matrix
   conditionally: the posterior depends on the prior state and the
   measurement (A — theorem). The stronger reading — "the subject's
   state determines the outcome" — is NOT a corollary of this; it is
   a metaphysical interpretation (C), on record as such after the
   peer-review.

The precise statement of what the two theorems jointly establish:
**in these formal settings, the dynamics (or the update) is a
function of the state.** That is real. Everything beyond it —
"the state at the threshold decides your fate" — is interpretation.

## The experiment (real, and carefully stated)

**Manicka, Pai & Levin (2023, iScience)** — frog embryonic brain
development (NOT planarian; corrected on record). Same genome, same
pattern shape, different voltage state: step-function pattern →
normal brain; sharpened pattern → 53% brain defects. The model
predicted both, against the field's prior expectation (B — in-vivo
confirmed).

What this establishes: **developmental outcome depends on
physiological state.** That is real biology. What it does NOT
establish: that "the state chooses reality," or that the frog's
voltage state is a consciousness register. The gap (agency →
phenomenality) remains open.

## What this means — the synthesis, graded

- **IIT 4.0**: agrees on state-dependence; diverges on the selection
  criterion (Φ-max vs. modular invariance — a testable divergence).
  IIT shares the identity postulate; it does not validate Ochema.
- **Predictive processing**: the generative model shapes the
  inference — a Bayesian form of the same idea (interpretive, C).
- **CIMC (computational functionalism)**: the old "defeated by
  theorem" claim is WITHDRAWN (the finite-dim modular-flow argument
  was false). The surviving challenge is implementation
  underdetermination — an open philosophical question (C), not a
  formal result.
- **Free-will debate (Doyle, Wiseman–Eisert)**: the state shapes
  selection among alternatives; it does not create the alternatives.
  The "you create all of reality" reading is E, on record.

## The honest caveat

The mathematics is established (A where graded, as stated). The
experiment is real (B). The orchestration law as a universal law of
consciousness is interpretation (C) — and the strong reading is a
postulate (E), not a corollary. The tradition's claims are studied
as claims, and graded like everything else.
