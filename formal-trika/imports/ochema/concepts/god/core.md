# Concept Core: God (the Christian notion, stated rigorously)

**concept_id: `con:god`** · version 1.0.0 · **live-updating**

Source of rigor: the Polkinghorne corpus (books + 21 divine-action papers,
`references/polkinghorne/`). This core does NOT claim God exists — it states
the Christian notion with maximal rigor, using Polkinghorne's formulation
as the canonical definition, and maps its structure onto the thesis's
machinery where the mapping is honest.

## The Formal Statement

**God (Christian): the self-authenticating ground of all that is — not an
entity among entities, but the source of all; the one whose deepest reality
is relational (perichoresis — the mutual exchange of love between the three
Persons); veiled in his encounter with finite humanity; possessing aseity
(being-in-itself), self-authenticating, the Subject of worship and not the
object of scrutiny.**

**Epistemic rule (Rahner):** the economic Trinity (God as experienced) IS
the immanent Trinity (God in himself). Approach from below: the experience
is trustworthy. This is the same structure as the thesis's Joint
Individuation Principle — knowledge recovered from experience, graded.

**Grade: A (structure — this is the rigorous definition of the Christian
notion, Polkinghorne's own; he is the best account of Christian
metaphysics) / C (identification with the thesis's machinery: the Veil,
the compatibility condition, kenosis-as-exclusion, love-as-coherence —
structural analogy, explicitly NOT a derivation of God).**

## The definition, unpacked (Polkinghorne's exact claims)

### 1. Not an entity among entities

> "He is not an entity among other entities, concerning whom one can say
> 'Lo, here' or 'Lo, there'... Rather, he is the source of all that is, the
> One omnipresent to every human experience. There is no possibility of
> identifying his presence by contrasting it with the different experience
> of his absence." (Reason and Reality, ch.4)

**Structural reading:** God is not a pole of the exclusion — he is the
exclusion's ground. He cannot be found by contrast because contrast is the
operation of finite registers. This is the thesis's exclusion operation
(E_i: Ω → (P_i, W_i)) seen from the other side: the subject pole and the
world pole are both WITHIN creation; the ground of the operation itself is
what theology calls God.

### 2. Aseity — self-authentication

> "If there is a God, who possesses aseity (being-in-itself), then he is
> self-authenticating; he does not call for evaluation by any other
> criteria." (Reason and Reality, ch.4)

**Structural reading:** the self-authenticating ground = the fixed point.
The thesis's subject index ι_i = the modular-invariant subalgebra — that
which is invariant under the bearer's own flow, self-constituted by the
state's own structure. God as aseity is the same form as the fixed point:
that which does not require an external criterion because it IS the
criterion's ground. (This is structural analogy, graded C — the thesis
does not and cannot derive God; it can say what structure a
self-authenticating ground must have.)

### 3. The Veil — God is necessarily veiled

> "God is necessarily veiled in his encounter with finite humanity and so
> it would not be altogether surprising if the veil proved impenetrable
> for many." (Reason and Reality, ch.4)

> "His acts will be veiled within the unpredictability of complex
> process. They may be discernible by faith but they will not be
> demonstrable by experiment." (Reason and Reality, ch.7)

**Structural reading:** THE VEIL. The thesis's veil = the compatibility
condition — the world-pole of the exclusion, what the register can
sustain. Polkinghorne's divine acts are veiled in complex process exactly
as the orchestration law is: the determination is the state's own law,
not a mechanism among mechanisms, so it cannot be experimentally
isolated. Two independent statements of the same epistemic structure:
what can be known is the structure, not the mechanism.

### 4. Perichoresis — God's deepest reality is relational

> "The one true God is constituted by the exchange of love between three
> divine Persons." (QPT, ch.4)

> "The three divine Persons are held to interpenetrate each other in the
> mutual exchange of love (a theological idea called 'perichoresis')."
> (QPT, ch.4)

> "Being as Communion... Reality is relational." (Zizioulas, via
> Polkinghorne QPT ch.4)

> "I believe that the true 'Theory of Everything' is not superstrings,
> as physicists are sometimes moved bombastically to proclaim, but it is
> actually trinitarian theology." (QPT, ch.4)

**Structural reading:** the exchange of love as the constitutive relation
= the thesis's love concept (con:love: coherence across the boundary —
the compatibility condition, met). The Trinity is the strongest statement
in the tradition that **relation, not substance, is the deepest reality**.
The thesis's invariants are relational structures (the moment-triad is
three moments in relation; the harmonic ladder is relations between
levels). Polkinghorne's Trinity = the harmonic ladder's qualitative
content at the divine scale: the octave of creation, redemption,
sanctification (Father/Son/Spirit) as the three moments — emergence,
persistence, withdrawal — of the divine action on creation.

### 5. Kenosis — the divine self-limitation

> "Love without power would correspond to a God who was a compassionate
> but impotent spectator of the history of the world. Power without love
> would [be tyranny]." (The Causality Distinction, Kenosis, and A Middle
> Way — the kenosis corpus)

**Structural reading:** kenosis = the exclusion operation, divine side.
The thesis's exclusion E_i is what makes the world-pole exist — the
contraction that constitutes the finite. God's self-limitation (letting
creation be free) is the same operation understood as voluntary: the
ground constituting the poles by not being everything. **This is the
deepest single alignment between Polkinghorne and the thesis: both make
self-limitation the fundamental operation.** (Thesis: exclusion as
determination. Polkinghorne: kenosis as love.)

## What this concept does NOT claim

1. **It does not derive God.** The thesis's machinery says nothing about
   the existence of a divine ground. The concept states: IF the Christian
   notion is true, its rigorous form is this; and that form shares
   structure with the thesis's machinery — which is why the tradition's
   best minds (Polkinghorne) independently describe God in structural
   terms that the thesis formalizes.
2. **It does not verify revelation.** The resurrection-as-pattern-
   duplication claim is REFUTED-as-stated (Swinburne; the subject is the
   fixed point, not the pattern) — see the Polkinghorne confrontation.
3. **It does not rehabilitate the God-of-the-gaps.** Polkinghorne's
   intrinsic gaps (chaos) are NOT the thesis's gaps — the thesis is
   causally closed; the orchestration law fills no gap because it IS the
   state's own law.

## Falsification / tests

- **Structural prediction:** any rigorous account of a self-authenticating
  ground will converge on the fixed-point form (self-constituted,
  invariant, non-contrastable). Polkinghorne's aseity is the first test
  case to pass.
- **The Veil prediction:** the divine/mechanism boundary will be
  undetectable in principle — any search for the "mechanism" of God's
  action (or the subject's) is a search for the Cartesian pineal gland:
  "condemned to failure" (Polkinghorne R&R ch.7). The thesis makes the
  same prediction for the subject pole.
- **Negative:** if a mechanism for God's action were found (a detectable
  divine input), the analogy fails on both sides — the thesis and
  Polkinghorne would be jointly wrong.
