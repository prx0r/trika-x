## 1. What spanda is (doctrine, minimum)

*Spanda-kārikā* (Vasugupta circle, ~9th c.): spanda = the throb/pulsation
of consciousness — not a physical vibration but the dynamic face of śiva.
Prakāśa (light) never sits still; its self-awareness (vimarśa) pulses as
**unmeṣa** (opening/evolution) and **nimeṣa** (closing/involution).
Sphurattā (shimmering-forth) is the same throb felt as the world arising.
Slogan: the universe is śiva blinking — seeming to close, never actually
shut. The sleeper, the dreamer and the waker are all modulations of one
pulse, never its absence.

## 2. What rasa is (doctrine, minimum)

Bharata's rasa-sūtra → Abhinavagupta's *Abhinavabhāratī*: rasa is
**camatkāra** — astonished relish — produced when emotion is universalised
(sādhāraṇīkaraṇa) past the private ego. Nine flavours (navarasa), śānta
(peace) supreme. *Tantrāloka*: supreme consciousness IS aesthetic relish
by nature — valence is not added to awareness, it is what awareness is.
Five blisses grade implicit self-presence → explicit self-recognition.
Our held synthesis (`rasa/RASAANALYSIS.md`): **Rasa = Geometry = Valence** —
TĀ doctrine × QRI symmetry-theory (pleasure = symmetry of the state) ×
Tymoczko (chords are points in an orbifold; felt quality IS location).
