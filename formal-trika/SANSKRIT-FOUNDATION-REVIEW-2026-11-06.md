Review base: trika-x `f42a2ea`; translation-work `e05e640e192a2ea5a317ba76b0f802f4f54e98cc`. This is an initial textual and implementation review, not a critical edition or independent scholarly sign-off. GRETIL's segment numbers for Tantrasāra prose are electronic locators, not verse numbers. The linked e-texts have noncommercial licence terms; links and short source references here do not clear rights for downstream reuse. | P0 — false passage coverage | `treeka.py` treated every locator listed under a work in `EVIDENCE.json` as formalized. Those lists are passage inventories. At the review base, 45 passages were marked true, while only 7 had direct theorem-entry references. Even a direct reference does not establish a checked theorem or a faithful textual mapping. Bare-locator essay joins also crossed work boundaries. | Version 2 reports exact work-and-locator registry references and retains `formalized: null` for unknown. Cross-work essay joins are scoped. | P0 — generated practice | The sample `vbt-d001` is `status: review`, but the pathway renderer emitted `PRACTICE` when its keyword classifier saw breath. Its R-tier gloss says “Stop breath”; [Vijñānabhairava 24](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_vijJAnabhairava.htm) refers to prāṇa, jīva, and the two arising points, without an explicit command to stop breath. Verse 25 and commentarial readings require separate examination. | Caution-bearing patterns fall to study and all assembled units remain study until an actual authorization boundary exists. A caller-supplied `approved` string is not evidence of review. The revised sample remains an unapproved R gloss. |
| P1 — 36 from compensating errors | [Tantrasāra 7.9–21, 8.16–17](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_abhinavagupta-tantrasAra.htm) distinguishes puruṣa, five kalā-led factors, māyā, and the five pure tattvas. Ananta appears among their **tattveśvaras** in 8.17, not as a sixth pure tattva. The old table omitted kalā from its five coverings and counted both Ananta and puruṣa among seven pure principles. These errors cancel numerically. | The table now counts puruṣa, the five coverings, māyā and five pure principles separately. `thirty_six` remains an arithmetic check of stipulated group sizes; it does not generate or verify 36 individual tattvas. |
| P1 — eleven faculties | In 7.9 the compound includes *tanmātra-pañcaka* and *akṣa-ekādaśa*: five subtle elements **and** eleven faculties. The old model instead summed five subtle elements, five gross elements and mind. Compare 8.73–77 for mind and the two groups of five faculties. | `eleven_wombed` now sums five cognitive faculties, five action faculties and mind. Its interpretation and source mapping still require human review. |
| P1 — follower classes versus means | Tantrasāra 8.17 lists five pure-course follower classes. It is not a definition of the three upāyas or a source for the model's numerical repetition rule. [Tantrasāra 1–3](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_abhinavagupta-tantrasAra.htm) distinguishes the three means and anupāya; śāmbhava must not simply be equated with anupāya. | The model and criteria remain experimental reconstructions. Removed 8.17 as direct evidence for those Boolean theorems; rater agreement alone cannot authorize means advice. |
| P1 — grace and readiness | Tantrasāra 8.35 and 8.39 mention power-descent and grace in particular contexts. Neither establishes the universal biconditional `flip = ready ∧ grace`. | Record the biconditional as a stipulated model definition. Its theorems do not establish that grace alone can never suffice, or that a compiler can assess readiness. |
| P1 — recognition and self | [Īśvarapratyabhijñākārikā 1.1.2–3 with Utpaladeva's vṛtti](https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/gretil/corpustei/transformations/html/sa_utpaladeva-IzvarapratyabhijJAkArikA-with-vRtti.htm) starts with an already established knowing and acting self, then describes recognition through the manifestation of powers. It is an argument within a larger debate, not evidence that any particular Boolean transition is the Sanskrit argument. | Preserve proposition, speaker, opposition, commentary and formal premise as separate objects. A proof about a transition becomes a claim about the passage only after a reviewed interpretation links them. |
| P1 — empty audit success | Source and decision audits reported success with zero works when their hard-coded checkout path was absent. Corrupt decision records were silently treated as empty. | Missing ledgers and corrupt decisions now fail; an existing file or marker is explicitly reported as presence, not a verified signature, witness or right. |

The current ipvv `two-sources-v1` gate counts supplied source-class strings. | The test now calls its evidence self-asserted and its claim unverified. | P0 — wrong-work and chapter joins | At `e05e640`, `sivasutra/data` contains 75 argmap units with `span-*` identifiers. The old join tried numeric verse matches before checking the named work, flattened chapter numbers, reversed descending ranges, and used a global first-match locator index. It also assigned a repeated local `par-*` locator to a different work. | Foreign or unknown chunk IDs, conflicting ranges and unqualified multi-chapter matches now stay `UNKNOWN`; an exact locator is resolved within its own work first. Human review of the underlying argmaps is still required. |

## Boundary for the wider system

1. Identify work, edition/transcription, passage and exact source bytes; record unresolved witness and rights fields explicitly.
2. Bind a versioned translation layer and argument map to those bytes. Record where its speaker and interpretive choices are disputed.
3. For each formal premise, record the precise source claim and a human-assessed mapping; identify added assumptions and alternatives. A passage locator is only a locator.
4. Record the named theorem, exact model and assumptions, toolchain and actual run. A finite-model result is conditional on its definitions.
5. Bind essay claims and teacher suggestions to those separate statuses. Source changes invalidate dependent assessments; missing review or rights remain unresolved. No result in this repository authorizes a practice or diagnosis.

The passage registry and this review are useful research inputs. They do not replace an edition-level philological review, a witness record, a proof-run receipt, or an authorized human decision.

named elements so that a reviewer can inspect the membership and the two
covering counts. It is an explicitly enumerated interpretation, not an
independent second textual witness or a derivation from the source.
