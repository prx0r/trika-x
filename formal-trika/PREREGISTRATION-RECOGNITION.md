## Kill and reopen criteria

- **Any Broken twin CHECKS** → the instrument is blind. Stop, fix, re-register.
- **Any T-tier contradiction** (e.g. a theorem denying a root-verse count) →
  the mapping is wrong. Fix the formalization; T1 immutable.
- **A rung generates a count the tradition does not state** → reported as OUR
  artifact (R), never as a discovery about the text, unless a Scholar snapshot
  signs the bridge premise.
- **Scope discipline:** every theorem carries its model size (64/4096/128…).
  A theorem about the finite model is not a theorem about minds.
